## Course Overview

This folder holds all problem sets, lab reports and quizzes for PHYS 141. It is part of the Fall 2026 submissions repo (see `../README.md`) — there is no separate repository here.

## Structure

One folder per teaching week (`week0/` … `week12/`). Each holds that
week's answer sheets alongside any lab data and analysis files.

| Week | Topic | Sheets |
|---|---|---|
| `week0/` | Measurement, units, coordinate systems & vectors | PS 0, Lab 0, Quiz 0 |
| `week1/` | Kinematics in 1D | PS 1, Lab 1, Quiz 1 |
| `week2/` | Kinematics in 2D — projectile & circular motion | PS 2, Lab 2, Quiz 2 |
| `week3/` | Newton's three laws of motion | PS 3, Lab 3, Quiz 3 |
| `week4/` | Work, energy & conservation of energy | PS 4, Lab 4, Quiz 4 |
| `week5/` | Momentum, impulse, collisions & centre of mass | PS 5, Lab 5, Quiz 5 |
| `week6/` | Rotational kinematics & dynamics | PS 6, Lab 6, Quiz 6 |
| `week7/` | Angular momentum & static equilibrium | PS 7, Lab 7, Quiz 7, **Midterm** |
| `week8/` | Simple harmonic motion & oscillators | PS 8, Lab 8, Quiz 8 |
| `week9/` | Waves — superposition & standing waves | PS 9, Lab 9, Quiz 9 |
| `week10/` | Sound — Doppler effect, resonance & decibels | PS 10, Lab 10, Quiz 10 |
| `week11/` | Thermodynamics I — temperature, heat, expansion | PS 11, Lab 11, Quiz 11 |
| `week12/` | Thermodynamics II — laws, entropy, heat engines | PS 12, Lab 12, Quiz 12, **Final Exam** |

41 sheets in total. Labs meet on the Thursday of their own week. Quizzes are given the Monday after their material, but each sheet sits in the week it covers, so `Quiz 12` sits in `week12/` and is sat on Monday 21 December.

**Q1 (2 pts).** Displacement is $\Delta x = x_f - x_i$.

$$\Delta x = 5 - (-3) = 5 + 3 = +8\ \text{m}.$$

**Answer: (B) 8 m**

**Q2 (2 pts).** On a position-time graph, the instantaneous velocity is the slope of the curve at that exact time; for a smooth curve this is the slope of the tangent.

**Answer: (C) the slope of the tangent line at $t$**

*Marks: 2 / 2*

---

**Q3 (3 pts).** Find $v(t)$ by differentiating $x(t) = 2t^2 - 8t + 3$:

$$v(t) = \frac{dx}{dt} = 4t - 8\quad (\text{m/s}).$$

At $t = 3$ s,

$$v(3) = 4(3) - 8 = 12 - 8 = \boxed{+4\ \text{m/s}}.$$

*Marks: 3 / 3*

---

**Q4 (2 pts).** Velocity is in the positive direction; acceleration is negative. When velocity and acceleration have opposite signs, the object is **slowing down**.

**Answer: (B) The object is slowing down**

*Marks: 2 / 2*

**Q5 (3 pts).** Accelerates from rest to $24$ m/s over distance $72$ m, constant $a$. Use $v^2 = v_0^2 + 2a\Delta x$:

$$24^2 = 0 + 2a(72) \;\Rightarrow\; 576 = 144a \;\Rightarrow\; a = \frac{576}{144} = \boxed{4.0\ \text{m/s}^2}.$$

*Marks: 3 / 3*

$$v^2 = v_0^2 + 2a\Delta x.$$

**Answer: (C)**

**Q7 (3 pts).** Thrown straight up from ground, $v_0 = 14$ m/s, $a = -9.81$ m/s², up positive. Maximum height when $v=0$:

$$v^2 = v_0^2 + 2a\Delta y \;\Rightarrow\; 0 = 14^2 + 2(-9.81)\Delta y \;\Rightarrow\; 2(9.81)\Delta y = 196$$
$$\Delta y = \frac{196}{19.62} = \boxed{10.0\ \text{m}}.$$

(Alternatively $v = v_0 + at$ gives $t=1.427$ s, then $\Delta y = v_0t + \tfrac{1}{2}at^2 = 19.978 - 9.989 = 9.99$ m.)

*Marks: 3 / 3*

**Q8 (2 pts).** Ball 1 falls for $t$, ball 2 for $t-1$ (s). Separation $s(t) = \tfrac{1}{2}gt^2 - \tfrac{1}{2}g(t-1)^2 = g\cdot t - g/2$ for $t\ge 1$ s — **linear in $t$**. The distance between them **increases at a constant rate** of $g \approx 9.8$ m/s.

**Answer: (C) Increases at a constant rate**

*Marks: 2 / 2*

**Q9 (3 pts).** $v(t)$ is a straight line through the origin, slope $4$ m/s².

(a) Acceleration is the slope: $a = \boxed{4\ \text{m/s}^2}$.

(b) For constant $a=4$ m/s² and $x(0)=0$, $v(t)=4t$ and $x(t)=\tfrac{1}{2}at^2 = \boxed{x(t)=2t^2\quad (\text{m})}$.

(c) Displacement from $t=0$ to $t=3$ s: $x(3)=2(9)=\boxed{18\ \text{m}}$ (area under $v$–$t$ line also gives 18 m).

*Marks: 3 / 3*

**Q10 (3 pts).** Thrown downward, origin at throw point, downward positive, $a=+9.81$ m/s².
$y(0)=0$, $v_0=+5.0$ m/s, $\Delta y = +25$ m.

$$\Delta y = v_0 t + \tfrac{1}{2}at^2 \;\Rightarrow\; 25 = 5.0t + 4.905t^2.$$

Rearrange:

$$4.905t^2 + 5.0t - 25 = 0.$$

Use quadratic:

$$t = \frac{-5.0 \pm \sqrt{5.0^2 - 4(4.905)(-25)}}{2(4.905)} = \frac{-5.0 \pm \sqrt{25 + 490.5}}{9.81} = \frac{-5.0 \pm \sqrt{515.5}}{9.81} = \frac{-5.0 \pm 22.71}{9.81}.$$

Positive root:

$$t = \frac{22.71 - 5.0}{9.81} = \frac{17.71}{9.81} = \boxed{1.80\ \text{s}}.$$

*Marks: 3 / 3*

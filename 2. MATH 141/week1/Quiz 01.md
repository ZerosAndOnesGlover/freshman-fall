**Q1 (4 pts). Domain of $f(x)=\frac{\sqrt{3-x}}{x^2-x-2}$.**

Numerator: $3-x \ge 0 \implies x \le 3$.
Denominator: $x^2 - x - 2 = (x-2)(x+1) \neq 0 \implies x \neq -1, x \neq 2$.
Combined: $(-\infty, -1) \cup (-1, 2) \cup (2, 3]$.

**Answer:** $(-\infty,-1)\cup(-1,2)\cup(2,3]$

*Marks: 4 / 4*

**Q2 (4 pts). $f(x)=\frac{1}{x+1}, g(x)=x^2-1$.**

(a) $(f\circ g)(x)=f(g(x))=f(x^2-1)=\frac{1}{(x^2-1)+1}=\frac{1}{x^2}$.
(b) Domain: $g(x)$ defined for all $\mathbb{R}$; need $g(x)\neq -1 \implies x^2-1 \neq -1 \implies x^2 \neq 0 \implies x \neq 0$. Thus $(-\infty,0)\cup(0,\infty)$.

**Answer:** (a) $\frac{1}{x^2}$, (b) $(-\infty,0)\cup(0,\infty)$

*Marks: 4 / 4*

**Q3 (3 pts). Solve $x^2 - 2x - 8 > 0$.**

Factor: $(x-4)(x+2)>0$. Critical points $-2,4$. Positive when $x<-2$ or $x>4$.

**Answer:** $(-\infty,-2)\cup(4,\infty)$

*Marks: 3 / 3*

**Q4 (3 pts). Exact values.**

(a) $\sin(5\pi/6)=\sin(\pi-\pi/6)=\frac{1}{2}$
(b) $\cos(-\pi/4)=\cos(\pi/4)=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$
(c) $\tan(2\pi/3)=\tan(\pi-\pi/3)=-\sqrt{3}$

**Answer:** (a) $1/2$, (b) $\sqrt{2}/2$, (c) $-\sqrt{3}$

**Q5 (3 pts). $2^{3x-1}=16$.**

$16=2^4 \implies 3x-1=4 \implies 3x=5 \implies x=\frac{5}{3}$.

**Answer:** $\frac{5}{3}$

*Marks: 3 / 3*

**Q6 (3 pts). $\ln(x+2)+\ln(x-1)=\ln(4)$.**

$\ln((x+2)(x-1))=\ln(4)\implies (x+2)(x-1)=4$. $x^2+x-2=4\implies x^2+x-6=0$. $(x+3)(x-2)=0\implies x=-3,2$. Domain requires $x>1$; $x=-3$ extraneous, $x=2$ valid.

**Answer:** $x=2$

*Marks: 3 / 3*

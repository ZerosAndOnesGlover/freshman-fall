# ECE 110 · Week 1 · Summary

**Topic —** Boolean algebra: axioms, theorems, De Morgan's Laws, duality, and canonical forms.
**Lectures —** L01 Boolean Axioms and Theorems; L02 De Morgan, Duality, and Canonical Forms.
**Work —** PS 1 (100), Lab 1 (100, every identity verified twice, by two independent Python checkers). **No quiz** — Quiz 1 is next week and covers this one.
**Takeaway —** The symbols were borrowed from arithmetic; the rules were not. A + BC = (A+B)(A+C) is a theorem here and false in arithmetic. Canonical is never minimal: Σm(1,3,5,6,7) goes from 17 gates to 2 — and the gate saving (8.5×) beats the literal saving (5×) because minimisation removed the inverters too.

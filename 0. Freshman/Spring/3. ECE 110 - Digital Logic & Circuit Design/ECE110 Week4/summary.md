# ECE 110 · Week 4 · Summary

**Topic —** Karnaugh maps: Gray-code adjacency, grouping, prime and essential implicants, don't-cares, and where maps stop.
**Lectures —** L01 Karnaugh Maps; L02 Minimization, Don't-Cares, and Beyond.
**Work —** PS 4 (100), Lab 4 (100, hand minimisation against a machine check), Quiz 3 (Wednesday, covers Week 3, ungraded).
**Takeaway —** A correct cover need not be minimal and nothing in the answer tells you: Σm(0,1,2,3,4,5,10,11,14,15) has a pairs-only cover that is completely correct at 20 literals against a minimum of 6, and minimising it takes 43 gates down to 7. Don't-cares are free literals but are a claim about the world, not the algebra.

# ECE 110 · Week 2 · Summary

**Topic —** Logic gates: the seven primitives, the digital abstraction, functional completeness, and gate-level design.
**Lectures —** L01 Logic Gates and CMOS Reality; L02 Functional Completeness and Gate-Level Design.
**Work —** PS 2 (100), Lab 2 (100, first hardware session + a gate-level simulation cross-check), Quiz 1 (Wednesday, covers Week 1, ungraded).
**Takeaway —** AND costs more than NAND in CMOS (6 transistors vs 4), so the algebra's primitives are not silicon's. NAND and NOR are each universal; {AND, OR} is not, and the monotonicity proof shows no construction exists rather than none being found. XOR is 4 NAND gates, XNOR is 4 NOR gates — duality, priced in silicon.

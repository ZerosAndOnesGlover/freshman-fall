# ECE 110 · Week 6 · Summary

**Topic —** The arithmetic logic unit, carry-lookahead, and the MIDTERM.
**Lectures —** L01 Building an ALU; L02 Carry-Lookahead and the Cost of Speed.
**Work —** PS 6 (100), Lab 6 (100, build an ALU and test all 2048 cases), Quiz 5 (Wednesday, covers Week 5, ungraded), **MIDTERM EXAM** (75 min, Weeks 0–5, 25%).
**Takeaway —** An ALU is an adder, some bitwise gates, and a mux — assembly, not invention; shift and compare cost no gates at all. Carry-lookahead finally solves Week 3's problem: measured 129 → 12 gate delays at 64 bits, a 10.8× speedup for about 2× the area, which is the difference between 7.7 clock periods (unusable) and 0.72 (fits).

# ECE 110 · Week 12 · Summary

**Topic —** Programmable logic: PLAs, PALs, FPGAs — plus review and the FINAL EXAM.
**Lectures —** L01 Programmable Logic — PLAs, PALs and FPGAs; L02 Review and the Road Ahead.
**Work —** PS 12 (100), Lab 12 (100), Quiz 11 (Wednesday, covers Week 11, ungraded — the last quiz), **FINAL EXAM** (Mon 19 Apr, 08:00, 120 min, comprehensive, 15%).
**Takeaway —** A ROM's cost grows as 2ⁿ because it decodes every input; a PLA's grows with the product terms you actually need — 524,288 against 2,560 at 16 inputs, which is the one place Week 4's minimisation pays off architecturally. An FPGA's k-LUT implements any k-input function at a cost independent of that function, which is the third route the course has taken to the lookup table. Real FPGAs harden carry chains because Week 3's serial dependency is universal.

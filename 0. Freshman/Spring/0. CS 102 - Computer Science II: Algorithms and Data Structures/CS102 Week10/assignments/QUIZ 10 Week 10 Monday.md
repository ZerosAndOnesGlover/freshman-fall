**Q1.** *(3)* State the **greedy-choice property**.

Be precise about whether the greedy choice must appear in *some* optimal solution or in *every* one,
and say why the distinction matters.

---

**Q2.** *(4)* Give the **exchange argument** proving that earliest-finish-time is optimal for activity
selection.

State what is exchanged, why the exchange is legal, and why the result is no worse.

---

**Q3.** *(3)* Two jobs $A$ and $B$ run adjacently on one machine.

- **(a)** *(2)* To minimise **total completion time**, which should go first, and by how much does
  swapping change the objective?
- **(b)** *(1)* To minimise **maximum lateness**, which should go first?

---

**Q4.** *(3)* Greedy by value-to-weight ratio is optimal for **fractional** knapsack and can achieve an
arbitrarily small fraction of the optimum for **0/1** knapsack.

Identify the step of the exchange argument that fails when items are indivisible.

---

**Q5.** *(4)* Huffman merges the two least frequent symbols.

- **(a)** *(3)* State the lemma this relies on, and give the expression whose sign proves it.
- **(b)** *(1)* Why is that expression necessarily $\le 0$?

---

**Q6.** *(3)* Huffman's average code length satisfies $H \le \bar\ell < H + 1$.

Give a distribution where the overhead is close to 1, and name the technique that does better.

## Problem 3: Motion (30 points)

A particle moves with position $s(t)=t^4-8t^3+18t^2$ metres, $t$ in seconds, on $0\le t\le4$.

**(a)** Find $v(t)$ and $a(t)$, factored. *(8 pts)*

**(b)** When is the particle at rest? *(6 pts)*

**(c)** Find the displacement and the total distance travelled over $[0,4]$. Justify from the factored
form of $v$ why they are or are not equal. *(8 pts)*

**(d)** At $t=2$, is the particle speeding up or slowing down? Justify by comparing signs. *(8 pts)*

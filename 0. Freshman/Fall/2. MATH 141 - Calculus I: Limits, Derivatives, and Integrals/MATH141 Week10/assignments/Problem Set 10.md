## Part C — Symmetry (10 pts)

**C1.** *(6 pts, 2 each)* Use symmetry (justify even/odd first) to evaluate:
- (a) $\displaystyle\int_{-4}^4 (x^5-3x^3+2x)\,dx$
- (b) $\displaystyle\int_{-1}^1 \frac{x^2\cos x}{1+x^4}\,dx$ — you do NOT need to fully evaluate; express as $2\times$ an integral from 0 to 1
- (c) $\displaystyle\int_{-\pi/4}^{\pi/4} \tan^3x\,dx$

**C2.** *(4 pts)* Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_{-2}^2 (x^6-4x^4+x^2+7)\,dx$ using symmetry to reduce your work.

## Part G — Conceptual (10 pts, 5 each)

**Q1.** Domain of $f(x) = \dfrac{\sqrt{3-x}}{x^2-x-2}$

- Numerator: $3 - x \geq 0 \Rightarrow x \leq 3$
- Denominator: $x^2 - x - 2 = (x-2)(x+1) \neq 0 \Rightarrow x \neq 2, x \neq -1$
- Combined: $x \leq 3$, $x \neq -1$, $x \neq 2$

**Domain:** $(-\infty, -1) \cup (-1, 2) \cup (2, 3]$

*Grading: 2 pts for correct numerator restriction, 1 pt for factoring denominator, 1 pt for correct final interval notation*

---

**Q2.** 

(a) $(f \circ g)(x) = f(g(x)) = f(x^2-1) = \dfrac{1}{(x^2-1)+1} = \dfrac{1}{x^2}$

(b) Domain: $g(x)$ defined for all $\mathbb{R}$; need $g(x) \neq -1$, i.e., $x^2 - 1 \neq -1 \Rightarrow x^2 \neq 0 \Rightarrow x \neq 0$.

**Domain:** $(-\infty, 0) \cup (0, \infty)$

*Grading: 2 pts (a), 2 pts (b)*

---

**Q3.** $x^2 - 2x - 8 > 0$

Factor: $(x-4)(x+2) > 0$. Critical points: $x = -2, 4$.

Sign chart: positive on $(-\infty, -2)$ and $(4, \infty)$.

**Answer:** $(-\infty, -2) \cup (4, \infty)$

*Grading: 1 pt factoring, 1 pt sign chart, 1 pt correct intervals*

---

**Q4.**

(a) $\sin(5\pi/6) = \sin(\pi - \pi/6) = \sin(\pi/6) = \boxed{1/2}$

(b) $\cos(-\pi/4) = \cos(\pi/4) = \boxed{\sqrt{2}/2}$

(c) $\tan(2\pi/3) = \tan(\pi - \pi/3) = -\tan(\pi/3) = \boxed{-\sqrt{3}}$

*Grading: 1 pt each*

**Q5.** $2^{3x-1} = 16 = 2^4$

$3x - 1 = 4 \Rightarrow 3x = 5 \Rightarrow \boxed{x = 5/3}$

*Grading: 1 pt converting 16, 1 pt equating exponents, 1 pt solving*

**Q6.** $\ln(x+2) + \ln(x-1) = \ln(4)$

$\ln[(x+2)(x-1)] = \ln 4$

$(x+2)(x-1) = 4$

$x^2 + x - 2 = 4$

$x^2 + x - 6 = 0$

$(x+3)(x-2) = 0 \Rightarrow x = -3$ or $x = 2$

**Check:** $x = -3$: $\ln(-1)$ undefined — extraneous. $x = 2$: $\ln(4) + \ln(1) = \ln(4)$ ✓

**Answer:** $\boxed{x = 2}$

*Grading: 1 pt combining logs, 1 pt solving quadratic, 1 pt checking/rejecting extraneous*

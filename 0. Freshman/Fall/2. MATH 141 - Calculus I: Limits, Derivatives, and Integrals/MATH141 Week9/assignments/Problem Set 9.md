## Problem 3: Reading an Accumulation Function (10 points)

$A(x)=\displaystyle\int_0^x f(t)\,dt$ where $f$ is continuous with $f>0$ on $(0,2)$, $f(2)=0$, and $f<0$ on
$(2,4)$. Where is $A$ increasing, decreasing, and maximal on $[0, 4]$? Justify each claim from the FTC, not
from a picture.

**(b)** $\displaystyle\int_1^4 \sqrt{x}\,dx$

**(c)** $\displaystyle\int_1^2\frac{dx}{x}$

**(d)** $\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi}\sin x\,dx$

## Problem 5: Signed and Geometric Area (10 points)

Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_0^{2\pi}\sin x\,dx$ and $\displaystyle\int_0^{2\pi}\lvert\sin x\rvert\,dx$. Explain the
difference.

## Problem 6: Find the Error (12 points)

A student writes $\displaystyle\int_{-1}^{1}\frac{dx}{x^2}=\Big[-\frac1x\Big]_{-1}^{1}=-1-1=-2$. Identify the
error, state which hypothesis of the FTC fails, and explain why a *negative* answer for a *positive*
integrand should have been the immediate warning sign.

---

## Problem 7: Filling a Tank (12 points)

Water flows into a tank at $r(t)=3t^2$ litres/min. The tank holds 10 L at $t=0$. Write the volume as a
function of $t$ using an integral, evaluate it, and check your answer by differentiating.

---

## Problem 8: What the FTC Really Says (12 points)

FTC 1 says differentiating an integral returns the integrand; FTC 2 says integrating a derivative returns
the net change. Explain how these are two readings of one statement, and why neither is obvious from the
definitions of derivative and integral.

## Part I: Functions and Domains (25 points)

**Problem 1.** *(10 points — 2 points each)*

For each function, state the natural domain in interval notation. Show the inequalities or equations you solve to find any restrictions.

**(a)** $f(x) = \dfrac{\sqrt{3-x}}{x^2 - 4}$

**(b)** $g(x) = \ln(x^2 - 5x + 6)$

**(c)** $h(x) = \sqrt{\dfrac{x+2}{x-1}}$

**(d)** $p(x) = \arcsin(2x - 1)$

**(e)** $q(x) = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{|x| - x}}$

---

**Problem 2.** *(8 points)*

Let $f(x) = \dfrac{x}{x-1}$.

**(a)** Find $f(f(x))$ and simplify completely. *(3 pts)*

**(b)** Find $f^{-1}(x)$. What do you notice about $f$ and $f^{-1}$? *(3 pts)*

**(c)** Find $f(f(f(x)))$. Can you spot the pattern? Prove it by induction (or strong inductive reasoning). *(2 pts)*

---

**Problem 3.** *(7 points)*

**(a)** Let $f(x) = x^2$ with domain $[0, \infty)$. *(4 pts)*
- Find $f^{-1}(x)$.
- State the domain and range of $f^{-1}$.
- Verify: $f^{-1}(f(x)) = x$ and $f(f^{-1}(x)) = x$ for all $x$ in the appropriate domains.

**(b)** Why does $f(x) = x^2$ on all of $\mathbb{R}$ fail to have an inverse? Explain both graphically and algebraically. *(3 pts)*

## Part II: Algebra and Inequalities (30 points)

**Problem 4.** *(12 points — 3 points each)*

Solve each equation. Find all real solutions.

**(a)** $\sqrt{x+5} - \sqrt{x-3} = 2$

**(b)** $x^4 - 5x^2 + 4 = 0$

**(c)** $|2x^2 - 3x| = 2$

**(d)** $\dfrac{2}{x-1} - \dfrac{3}{x+2} = \dfrac{4}{x^2+x-2}$

---

**Problem 5.** *(12 points — 4 points each)*

Solve each inequality. Express your answer in interval notation. Show sign charts where applicable.

**(a)** $\dfrac{x^2 - 1}{x^2 - 4} \geq 0$

**(b)** $|x^2 - 2x - 3| < 5$

**(c)** $\dfrac{x^2 - 3x}{x^2 + x - 2} \leq 1$

---

**Problem 6.** *(6 points)*

Perform the following algebraic manipulations. These exact steps appear in derivative calculations.

**(a)** Simplify: $\dfrac{\sqrt{x+h} - \sqrt{x}}{h}$ by rationalizing the numerator. What does this expression approach as $h \to 0$? *(3 pts)*

**(b)** Simplify: $\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{(x+h)^2} - \dfrac{1}{x^2}}{h}$. Expand and cancel $h$ from numerator and denominator. *(3 pts)*

## Part III: Exponentials and Logarithms (25 points)

**Problem 7.** *(10 points — 2.5 points each)*

Solve for $x$ exactly (not decimal approximations).

**(a)** $e^{2x} - 4e^x + 3 = 0$

**(b)** $\log_3(x+4) - \log_3(x) = 2$

**(c)** $3^x \cdot 9^{x-1} = 27^{2x}$

**(d)** $\ln(x+1) - \ln(x-1) = 1$

---

**Problem 8.** *(8 points)*

A certain medication is metabolized exponentially. Its concentration in the bloodstream follows:
$$C(t) = C_0 e^{-kt}$$
where $C_0$ is the initial concentration and $t$ is in hours.

**(a)** The concentration halves every 4 hours. Find $k$ exactly. *(2 pts)*

**(b)** If the initial concentration is 100 mg/L, find when the concentration drops below 10 mg/L. *(3 pts)*

**(c)** A "therapeutic window" requires $C(t) \geq 15$ mg/L. How long is the drug effective? *(3 pts)*

---

**Problem 9.** *(7 points)*

**(a)** Prove the change of base formula: $\log_a x = \dfrac{\ln x}{\ln a}$ *(3 pts)*

**(b)** Using the change of base formula, evaluate $\log_8 32$ without a calculator. *(2 pts)*

**(c)** Prove that $\log_a b \cdot \log_b c \cdot \log_c a = 1$ for valid bases $a, b, c$. *(2 pts)*

## Part IV: Trigonometry (20 points)

**Problem 10.** *(8 points)*

Without a calculator, find the exact value of each expression.

**(a)** $\cos\left(\dfrac{7\pi}{6}\right)$ *(1 pt)*

**(b)** $\tan\left(-\dfrac{3\pi}{4}\right)$ *(1 pt)*

**(c)** $\sin\left(\dfrac{17\pi}{3}\right)$ *(2 pts)*

**(d)** $\arctan\left(\tan\left(\dfrac{5\pi}{4}\right)\right)$ *(2 pts)*

**(e)** $\cos(\arcsin(3/5))$ *(2 pts)*

---

**Problem 11.** *(6 points)*

Prove each identity. Start from one side and transform it to the other — do not manipulate both sides simultaneously.

**(a)** $\dfrac{\tan\theta + \cot\theta}{\sec\theta\csc\theta} = 1$ *(3 pts)*

**(b)** $\cos(A - B) - \cos(A + B) = 2\sin A \sin B$ *(3 pts)*

---

**Problem 12.** *(6 points)*

**(a)** Find all solutions in $[0, 2\pi)$: $\sin(2\theta) = \cos\theta$ *(3 pts)*

**(b)** Find all solutions in $[0, 2\pi)$: $2\cos^2\theta + 3\sin\theta = 3$ *(3 pts)*

## Challenge Problems (Bonus - up to 10 points)

*These will not raise your grade above 100, but are excellent preparation for the difficulty of the course. Attempt them only after completing the main assignment.*

**Bonus 1.** *(4 points)*

Prove the **triangle inequality** rigorously: $|a + b| \leq |a| + |b|$ for all $a, b \in \mathbb{R}$.  
Then prove the **reverse triangle inequality**: $\big| |a| - |b| \big| \leq |a - b|$.  
*Hint for forward: Use $|x|^2 = x^2$. For reverse: apply forward inequality with $a = (a-b) + b$.*

**Bonus 2.** *(3 points)*

A function $f: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ satisfies $f(x + y) = f(x) + f(y)$ for all $x, y \in \mathbb{R}$ (called **Cauchy's functional equation**).

- Prove that $f(0) = 0$.
- Prove that $f(nx) = nf(x)$ for all positive integers $n$.
- Prove that $f(rx) = rf(x)$ for all rational numbers $r$.
- What function does this force $f$ to be, assuming $f$ is continuous?

**Bonus 3.** *(3 points)*

Without a calculator, determine which is larger: $e^\pi$ or $\pi^e$.  
Provide a complete mathematical proof (not just a decimal approximation argument).  
*Hint: Consider the function $g(x) = \ln x / x$ and find its behavior.*

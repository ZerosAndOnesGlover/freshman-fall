## Problem 1: Domains (12 points)

State the natural domain of each function in interval notation. Show the inequalities or equations you solve to find the restrictions. *(6 pts each)*

**(a)** $f(x) = \dfrac{\sqrt{3-x}}{x^2 - 4}$

**(b)** $g(x) = \ln(x^2 - 5x + 6)$

---

## Problem 2: A Function That Undoes Itself (12 points)

Let $f(x) = \dfrac{x}{x-1}$.

**(a)** Find $f(f(x))$ and simplify completely. *(6 pts)*

**(b)** Find $f^{-1}(x)$. What do you notice about $f$ and $f^{-1}$, and how does part (a) explain it? *(6 pts)*

---

## Problem 3: Equations (12 points)

Find all real solutions. Check every candidate in the original equation. *(6 pts each)*

**(a)** $\sqrt{x+5} - \sqrt{x-3} = 2$

**(b)** $x^4 - 5x^2 + 4 = 0$

---

## Problem 4: Inequalities (12 points)

Solve each inequality and give the answer in interval notation. Show a sign chart where one is needed. *(6 pts each)*

**(a)** $\dfrac{x^2 - 1}{x^2 - 4} \geq 0$

**(b)** $|x^2 - 2x - 3| < 5$

---

## Problem 5: Rationalizing (10 points)

Simplify $\dfrac{\sqrt{x+h} - \sqrt{x}}{h}$ by rationalizing the numerator, until no $h$ is left in the denominator. Then say what the simplified expression equals when you put $h = 0$.

*This exact step reappears in Week 3, when we compute derivatives.*

---

## Problem 6: Exponential and Logarithmic Equations (12 points)

Solve for $x$ exactly (no decimal approximations). *(6 pts each)*

**(a)** $e^{2x} - 4e^x + 3 = 0$

**(b)** $\ln(x+1) - \ln(x-1) = 1$

---

## Problem 7: Half-Life (12 points)

A medication's concentration in the bloodstream is $C(t) = C_0 e^{-kt}$, with $t$ in hours.

**(a)** The concentration halves every 4 hours. Find $k$ exactly. *(5 pts)*

**(b)** If $C_0 = 100$ mg/L, find when the concentration first drops below 10 mg/L. Give the exact value and a decimal to two places. *(7 pts)*

---

## Problem 8: Trigonometry (18 points)

Without a calculator: *(6 pts each)*

**(a)** Find the exact value of $\sin\left(\dfrac{17\pi}{3}\right)$.

**(b)** Find the exact value of $\cos(\arcsin(3/5))$.

**(c)** Find all solutions of $\sin(2\theta) = \cos\theta$ in $[0, 2\pi)$.

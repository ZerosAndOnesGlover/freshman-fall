## Section A: Algebra (20 questions)

**A1.** Simplify: $(2x^3y^{-2})^3 \cdot \dfrac{x^{-1}}{4y}$

**A2.** Factor completely: $2x^3 - 50x$

**A3.** Solve: $x^2 - 3x - 10 = 0$

**A4.** Solve: $|2x + 3| = 7$

**A5.** Simplify: $\dfrac{x^2 - 9}{x^2 + 3x} \cdot \dfrac{x^2 + 5x}{x^2 - x - 6}$

**A6.** Rationalize the denominator: $\dfrac{3}{\sqrt{5} - \sqrt{2}}$

**A7.** Simplify: $\dfrac{x^{-1} - y^{-1}}{x^{-1} + y^{-1}}$

**A8.** Complete the square: $x^2 + 6x - 7$. Write in the form $(x + a)^2 + b$.

**A9.** Solve the inequality: $x^2 - x - 6 \leq 0$. Write in interval notation.

**A10.** The solutions to $x^2 + px + q = 0$ are $x = 3$ and $x = -5$. Find $p$ and $q$.

**A11.** Simplify: $\dfrac{\sqrt{x+h} - \sqrt{x}}{h}$ after rationalizing the numerator.

**A12.** Solve: $\dfrac{x+1}{x-2} > 3$. Write in interval notation.

**A13.** If $f(x) = 3x - 1$ and $g(x) = x^2 + 2$, find $(f \circ g)(x)$ and $(g \circ f)(x)$.

**A14.** For $f(x) = \dfrac{2x+1}{x-3}$, find $f^{-1}(x)$.

**A15.** Solve the system: $\begin{cases} x^2 + y = 6 \\ x + y = 2 \end{cases}$

**A16.** Expand and simplify: $(x+h)^3 - x^3$. Factor out $h$.

**A17.** Find all real roots of $x^4 - 13x^2 + 36 = 0$.

**A18.** Solve: $\sqrt{2x-1} + 3 = x$

**A19.** If $P(x) = 2x^3 - 3x^2 - 11x + 6$, find all rational roots and factor completely.

**A20.** Perform polynomial long division: $\dfrac{x^3 - 2x^2 + 3x - 1}{x - 1}$

## Section B: Functions (10 questions)

**B1.** Find the domain of $f(x) = \dfrac{\sqrt{x-2}}{x^2-9}$. Write in interval notation.

**B2.** Determine whether $f(x) = x^3 - x$ is even, odd, or neither.

**B3.** Given $f(x) = x^2$ on domain $[0, \infty)$, find $f^{-1}(x)$.

**B4.** The graph of $f(x)$ is given. Describe how to obtain the graph of $f(-x+1) + 2$ from the graph of $f(x)$ using transformations. List each transformation in order.

**B5.** Find the average rate of change of $f(x) = x^3$ from $x = -1$ to $x = 2$.

**B6.** If $f(2) = 5$ and $g(5) = -1$, find $(g \circ f)(2)$.

**B7.** Does $\{(1,2), (3,4), (5,2), (7,8)\}$ represent a function? Is it one-to-one?

**B8.** Find a formula for the piecewise function with:
- $f(x) = x + 1$ for $x < 0$
- $f(x) = 1$ for $x = 0$
- $f(x) = x^2 - 1$ for $x > 0$

Evaluate $f(-3)$, $f(0)$, $f(2)$.

**B9.** A function $f$ has domain $[-3, 5]$ and range $[0, 8]$. What is the domain of $f^{-1}$? What is its range?

**B10.** Find the intersection points of $y = x^2 - 1$ and $y = 2x + 2$.

## Section C: Exponentials and Logarithms (10 questions)

**C1.** Simplify: $\log_3 81 - \log_3 27$

**C2.** Solve: $4^{x-1} = 8^x$

**C3.** Write $\ln\left(\dfrac{x^3\sqrt{y}}{z^2}\right)$ in terms of $\ln x$, $\ln y$, $\ln z$.

**C4.** Solve: $\log_2(x+3) + \log_2(x-1) = 5$

**C5.** If $\ln x = 3$ and $\ln y = 5$, find $\ln\left(\dfrac{x^2}{y^3}\right)$.

**C6.** An investment grows at 6% per year, compounded continuously. How long does it take to double?

**C7.** Evaluate: $e^{3\ln 2}$

**C8.** Solve: $e^{2x} - 6e^x + 5 = 0$

**C9.** Convert to a single logarithm: $3\log x - \dfrac{1}{2}\log(x+1) + \log 5$

**C10.** If $\log_a 2 = 0.3$ and $\log_a 3 = 0.5$, find $\log_a 72$.

## Section D: Trigonometry (10 questions)

**D1.** Without a calculator, find $\sin(5\pi/6)$, $\cos(5\pi/6)$, $\tan(5\pi/6)$.

**D2.** Find all $\theta \in [0, 2\pi)$ satisfying $\sin\theta = -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$.

**D3.** Prove: $\dfrac{\cos^2\theta - \sin^2\theta}{\cos\theta + \sin\theta} = \cos\theta - \sin\theta$

**D4.** Find $\sin(A+B)$ given $\sin A = 3/5$, $\cos B = 5/13$ where $A$ and $B$ are both in Quadrant I.

**D5.** Simplify: $\dfrac{1 - \cos(2\theta)}{2}$

**D6.** Solve for $x \in [0, 2\pi)$: $\cos(2x) = \cos x$

**D7.** A right triangle has hypotenuse 13 and one leg 5. Find all six trig functions of the smallest angle.

**D8.** Convert $315°$ to radians. Convert $\dfrac{7\pi}{4}$ to degrees.

**D9.** Find the exact value of $\tan(\arcsin(4/5))$.

**D10.** Prove the identity: $\sec^2\theta - \tan^2\theta = 1$

## Section A: Algebra (4 questions)

**A1.** Solve: $x^2 - 3x - 10 = 0$

**A2.** Solve: $|2x + 3| = 7$

**A3.** Complete the square: $x^2 + 6x - 7$. Write in the form $(x + a)^2 + b$.

**A4.** Solve the inequality: $x^2 - x - 6 \leq 0$. Write in interval notation.

---

## Section B: Functions (2 questions)

**B1.** Find the domain of $f(x) = \dfrac{\sqrt{x-2}}{x^2-9}$. Write in interval notation.

**B2.** Find the average rate of change of $f(x) = x^3$ from $x = -1$ to $x = 2$.

---

## Section C: Exponentials and Logarithms (3 questions)

**C1.** Simplify: $\log_3 81 - \log_3 27$

**C2.** Solve: $4^{x-1} = 8^x$

**C3.** Solve: $\log_2(x+3) + \log_2(x-1) = 5$

---

## Section D: Trigonometry (3 questions)

**D1.** Without a calculator, find $\sin(5\pi/6)$, $\cos(5\pi/6)$, $\tan(5\pi/6)$.

**D2.** Find all $\theta \in [0, 2\pi)$ satisfying $\sin\theta = -\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$.

**D3.** Simplify: $\dfrac{1 - \cos(2\theta)}{2}$

---

## Self-Assessment Answer Key

*Check your answers honestly. Be very harsh on yourself — "I kind of got it" doesn't count.*

**A1.** $x = 5$ or $x = -2$

**A2.** $x = 2$ or $x = -5$

**A3.** $(x+3)^2 - 16$

**A4.** $[-2, 3]$

**B1.** $[2, 3) \cup (3, \infty)$

**B2.** $\dfrac{2^3 - (-1)^3}{2-(-1)} = \dfrac{9}{3} = 3$

**C1.** $4 - 3 = 1$

**C2.** $2^{2(x-1)} = 2^{3x} \Rightarrow 2x-2 = 3x \Rightarrow x = -2$

**C3.** $x = 5$ *(Check: $x=-9$ is invalid, since the logarithms need $x > 1$)*

**D1.** $\sin(5\pi/6) = 1/2$; $\cos(5\pi/6) = -\sqrt{3}/2$; $\tan(5\pi/6) = -1/\sqrt{3}$

**D2.** $\theta = 4\pi/3$ and $\theta = 5\pi/3$

**D3.** $\sin^2\theta$ *(from $\cos(2\theta) = 1 - 2\sin^2\theta$)*

## Learning Objectives

1. State the structure of the course: limits → derivatives ↔ integrals, joined by the FTC
2. Identify which theorem applies to a given existence question
3. Construct a Taylor polynomial and interpret its error term
4. Recognise the tangent line as $T_1$ and Taylor's theorem as the MVT generalised
5. Diagnose your own weak areas and revise them specifically

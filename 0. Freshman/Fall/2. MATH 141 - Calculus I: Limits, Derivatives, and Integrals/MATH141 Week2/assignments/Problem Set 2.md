## Problem 1: The Definition (10 points)

State the three-part definition of continuity at $a$. Then give a function that fails each part in turn
(three examples, one per part).

---

## Problem 2: Where Is It Continuous? (12 points)

Determine where each function is continuous. *(4 pts each)*

**(a)** $\dfrac{x+1}{x^2-4}$ **(b)** $\sqrt{x-3}$ **(c)** $\dfrac{\sin x}{x}$

---

## Problem 3: Choosing a Value (10 points)

Find $k$ so that $f(x)=\begin{cases}\dfrac{x^2-9}{x-3},&x\ne3\\ k,&x=3\end{cases}$ is continuous at $3$.

---

## Problem 4: Classifying Discontinuities (16 points)

Classify the discontinuity at the stated point as removable, jump, infinite or essential, and give the
reason. *(4 pts each)*

**(a)** $\dfrac{x^2-4}{x-2}$ at $2$ **(b)** $\dfrac{x+2}{x-2}$ at $2$ **(c)** $\dfrac{\lvert x\rvert}{x}$ at $0$
**(d)** $\cos(1/x)$ at $0$

---

## Problem 5: Finding Every Discontinuity (12 points)

For $f(x)=\dfrac{x^2-9}{x^2-3x}$, find **every** discontinuity and classify each.

---

## Problem 6: The Intermediate Value Theorem (14 points)

*(7 pts each)*

**(a)** Show that $x^3-x-2$ has a root in $[1,2]$. State precisely what the IVT does and does not tell you.

**(b)** Show that $\cos x=x$ has a solution in $[0,1]$.

---

## Problem 7: Bisection by Hand (14 points)

Perform three bisection steps on $x^3-x-2$ over $[1,2]$. Tabulate the bracket, the midpoint, and
$f(\text{mid})$ at each step, and give the final bracket.

---

## Problem 8: Why It Is Deep (12 points)

Explain why the IVT is **false over $\mathbb{Q}$**, with an explicit example. What property of
$\mathbb{R}$ rescues it?

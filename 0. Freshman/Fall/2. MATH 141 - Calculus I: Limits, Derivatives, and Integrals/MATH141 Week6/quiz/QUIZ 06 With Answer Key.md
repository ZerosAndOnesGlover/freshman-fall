**Q1.** $x^2y+y^3=10$

$2xy+x^2y'+3y^2y'=0$

$y'(x^2+3y^2)=-2xy$

$y'=\dfrac{-2xy}{x^2+3y^2}$

*Grading: 2 pts differentiating (chain rule on $y$ terms + product rule on $x^2y$), 2 pts solving for $y'$*

---

**Q2.** $y=\ln(x^2+\tan x)$

$$y'=\frac{2x+\sec^2x}{x^2+\tan x}$$

*Grading: 2 pts chain rule setup, 2 pts correct derivative of inner function*

---

**Q3.** $y=x^{2x}$

$\ln y=2x\ln x$

$\dfrac{y'}{y}=2\ln x+2x\cdot\dfrac1x=2\ln x+2$

$y'=x^{2x}(2\ln x+2)$

*Grading: 1 pt taking log, 1 pt implicit differentiation, 1 pt solving for $y'$, 1 pt substituting back*

---

**Q4.** $y=\arctan(x^2)$

$$y'=\frac{2x}{1+x^4}$$

*Grading: 2 pts chain rule setup, 2 pts correct answer*

**Q5.** $V=s^3$; $\dfrac{ds}{dt}=3$ cm/s; find $\dfrac{dV}{dt}$ at $s=4$.

$\dfrac{dV}{dt}=3s^2\dfrac{ds}{dt}=3(16)(3)=144$ cm³/s

*Grading: 1 pt equation, 2 pts differentiating correctly, 1 pt correct substitution/answer*

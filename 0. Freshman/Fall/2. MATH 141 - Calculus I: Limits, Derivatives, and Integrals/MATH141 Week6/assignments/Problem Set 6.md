## Problem 4: The MVT (10 points)

## Problem 5: The Cyclist (10 points)

Two towns are connected by a mountain road 45 km long. A cyclist covers the distance in exactly 1.5 hours.
Prove, using the MVT, that at some instant the cyclist's speed was exactly 30 km/h. State the theorem's
hypotheses and explain why they apply to this physical situation.

## Problem 6: An Inequality from the MVT (10 points)

Use the MVT to prove that for all $x < y$: $\;|\sin y - \sin x| \leq |y - x|$.

*(Hint: apply the MVT to $f(t) = \sin t$ on $[x,y]$, and use $|\cos c| \leq 1$.)*

## Problem 7: L'Hôpital's Rule (24 points)

Evaluate each limit. Name the indeterminate form before applying L'Hôpital's Rule, and convert to $0/0$ or
$\infty/\infty$ first where needed. *(8 pts each)*

**(a)** $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0}\frac{e^x-1-x}{x^2}$

**(b)** $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0^+}x\ln x$

**(c)** $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(1+\frac{3}{x}\right)^{x}$

## Problem 8: A Zero Derivative (10 points)

A student says: "If $f'(c) = 0$, then $f$ has a local extremum at $c$." Give a specific counterexample and
explain, using the definitions from Monday's lecture, exactly why it fails to be a local extremum.

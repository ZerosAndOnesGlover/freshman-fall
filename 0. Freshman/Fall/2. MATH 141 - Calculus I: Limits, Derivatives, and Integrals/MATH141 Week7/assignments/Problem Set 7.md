## Problem 3: The Open Box (12 points)

A rectangular box with a square base and open top must have volume $32{,}000\ \text{cm}^3$. Find the
dimensions that minimize the amount of material used.

---

## Problem 4: Rectangle in an Ellipse (12 points)

A rectangle is inscribed in the ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{16}+\dfrac{y^2}{9}=1$ with sides parallel to the axes.
Find the dimensions of the rectangle with maximum area.

---

## Problem 5: The Window (12 points)

A window is a rectangle topped by a semicircle. Its outer boundary — the bottom and two sides of the
rectangle plus the semicircular arc — is $10$ m long. (The diameter where the semicircle meets the rectangle
is inside the window and is not counted.) Find the dimensions that maximize the area of the window.

---

## Problem 6: When $f''(c) = 0$ (14 points)

Consider $f_1(x)=x^4$, $f_2(x)=-x^4$ and $f_3(x)=x^3$.

**(a)** Show that $f'(0)=0$ and $f''(0)=0$ for all three. *(4 pts)*

**(b)** Classify $x=0$ for each (local min, local max, or neither) using the First Derivative Test. *(6 pts)*

**(c)** What do (a) and (b) together say about the Second Derivative Test when $f''(c)=0$? *(4 pts)*

## Problem 7: Verifying an Optimum (10 points)

In an optimization problem, why is it not enough to find where $f'(x)=0$ and declare that the answer?
Describe the verification step(s) required, naming the tests from Weeks 6–7.

## Problem 1: Area Between Curves (10 points)

Find the area enclosed by $y=x^2-4$ and $y=4-x^2$.

---

## Problem 2: Integrating in $y$ (14 points)

Find the area bounded by $x=y^2$ and $x=y+2$. Do it in $y$, then explain in two sentences why doing it in
$x$ would require two integrals.

---

## Problem 3: A Disk Volume (10 points)

Find the volume when $y=x^2$ on $[0,2]$ is revolved about the $x$-axis.

---

## Problem 4: The Cone (12 points)

Derive the volume of a cone of radius $r$ and height $h$ by revolving a line.

---

## Problem 5: Known Cross-Sections (12 points)

A solid has base the disk $x^2+y^2\le4$, with square cross-sections perpendicular to the $x$-axis. Find its
volume. State carefully what the side length is.

---

## Problem 6: Shells (10 points)

Find the volume when $y=x^2$ on $[0,3]$ is revolved about the $y$-axis.

---

## Problem 7: Shells Again (10 points)

Find the volume when $y=\sqrt x$ on $[0,4]$ is revolved about the $y$-axis.

---

## Problem 8: Work (12 points)

A spring obeys $F(x)=200x$ N. Find the work done stretching it from its natural length to $0.3$ m, and
state the general formula for a linear spring.

---

## Problem 9: One Idea, Four Uses (10 points)

In one sentence each, state what a single slice contributes for: area between curves, a volume by disks, a
volume by shells, and displacement. Then state what all four share.

## Problem 7: Related Rates (24 points)

For each: draw a diagram, label variables, identify the given and wanted rates, write the equation
relating the variables, differentiate with respect to time, then substitute and solve. *(12 pts each)*

**(a)** A spherical snowball melts so that its volume decreases at $1\ \text{cm}^3/\text{min}$. How fast is the
radius decreasing when $r = 5\ \text{cm}$? How fast is the surface area decreasing at that moment?

**(b)** A water trough is $10\ \text{m}$ long. Its cross-section is an isosceles triangle with width $2\ \text{m}$
at the top and depth $1\ \text{m}$. Water is poured in at $0.5\ \text{m}^3/\text{min}$. How fast is the water
level rising when the depth is $0.3\ \text{m}$?

*(Hint: find the water's width as a function of its depth by similar triangles.)*

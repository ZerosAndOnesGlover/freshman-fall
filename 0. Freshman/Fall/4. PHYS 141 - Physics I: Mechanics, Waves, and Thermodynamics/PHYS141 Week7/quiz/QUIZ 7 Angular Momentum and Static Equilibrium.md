# PHYS 141 · Quiz 7
## Angular Momentum & Static Equilibrium

**Administered:** Monday 16 November 2026, 14:00 (start of lecture) · Week 8, the Monday after this material is covered — covers Week 7.
**Time limit:** 20 minutes | **Format:** Closed book
**Formula sheet:** L=mvr sinφ (particle); L=Iω (rigid body); τ_net=dL/dt; conservation: I_iω_i=I_fω_f; equilibrium: ΣF=0, Στ=0

---

**1.** (2 pts) A 0.40 kg particle moves in a circle of radius 2.0 m at 5.0 m/s. Find its angular momentum about the center.

---

**2.** (2 pts) A skater pulls her arms in while spinning on frictionless ice. Which of the following is conserved?

A) Kinetic energy only
B) Angular momentum only
C) Both kinetic energy and angular momentum
D) Neither

---

**3.** (3 pts) A disk with I=0.50 kg·m² spins at ω=6.0 rad/s. A second identical disk, initially at rest, is dropped onto it and sticks (same axis). Find the common final angular velocity.

---

**4.** (2 pts) For static equilibrium, which of the following must be true?

A) ΣF=0 only
B) Στ=0 only
C) Both ΣF=0 and Στ=0
D) Neither, only that the object appears stationary

---

**5.** (3 pts) A skater has I=6.0 kg·m² and ω=2.0 rad/s with arms extended. Pulling arms in reduces I to 2.0 kg·m². Find the new angular velocity.

---

**6.** (2 pts) If a system is in translational equilibrium (ΣF=0), the net torque about any point is:

A) Always zero automatically
B) The same value regardless of which point you choose
C) Always different depending on the point chosen
D) Undefined

---

**7.** (3 pts) A uniform beam of length 6.0 m and weight 300 N is supported at both ends. A 200 N weight is placed 2.0 m from the left end. Find the force exerted by the left support.

---

**8.** (2 pts) A comet's angular momentum about the Sun is conserved throughout its orbit because:

A) The comet's mass is constant
B) The gravitational force is always directed along the line to the Sun (zero torque about the Sun)
C) The comet's speed is constant
D) The Sun exerts no force at all on the comet

---

**9.** (3 pts) A torque of 12 N·m acts on a system for 5.0 s, changing its angular momentum. If the initial angular momentum was 10 kg·m²/s, find the final angular momentum.

---

**10.** (3 pts) A 3.0 m uniform ladder (weight 150 N) leans against a frictionless wall at 60° from horizontal. Find the normal force from the wall. (sin 60° = 0.866, cos 60° = 0.500.)

---

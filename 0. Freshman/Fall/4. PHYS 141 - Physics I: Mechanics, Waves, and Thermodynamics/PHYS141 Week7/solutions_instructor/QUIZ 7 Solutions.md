**7.** **Choose the pivot at the right support (x = 6.0 m).** This eliminates N_right from the torque
equation, leaving N_left as the only unknown — which is the whole point of the free pivot choice
from Q6.

Lever arms measured from the pivot:
- N_left: upward at x = 0, arm = 6.0 m
- beam weight 300 N: at the centre, x = 3.0 m, arm = 6.0 − 3.0 = 3.0 m
- load 200 N: at x = 2.0 m, arm = 6.0 − 2.0 = 4.0 m

Στ = N_left(6.0) − 300(3.0) − 200(4.0) = 0
N_left(6.0) = 900 + 800 = 1700
**N_left = 1700/6.0 = 283.3 N**

*Cross-check:* pivoting at the left support instead gives N_right(6.0) = 300(3.0) + 200(2.0) = 1300,
so N_right = 216.7 N. Their sum is 283.3 + 216.7 = **500 N**, which is the total weight — confirming
ΣF = 0 as required. Award full credit for either pivot; reward students who verify with the second.

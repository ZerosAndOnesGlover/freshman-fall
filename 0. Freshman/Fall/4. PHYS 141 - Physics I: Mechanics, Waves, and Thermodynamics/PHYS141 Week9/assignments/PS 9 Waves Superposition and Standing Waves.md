## Part A — Wave Properties (30 points)

**1.** *(10 pts)* A wave has $f = 512$ Hz and $\lambda = 0.664$ m. Find $v$, $T$, $k$, and $\omega$.

**2.** *(10 pts)* A string has $\mu = 8.00$ g/m under 120 N of tension. Find the wave speed. What
tension would be needed to double it? Comment on whether that is practical.

**3.** *(10 pts)* A wave is described by $y = (0.0200~\text{m})\sin(4.00x - 60.0t)$, SI units. State
$A$, $\lambda$, $k$, $f$, $T$, $v$, and the direction of travel.

## Part B — Superposition and Interference (20 points)

**4.** *(10 pts)* Two waves of amplitude 4.00 cm interfere with a phase difference of $\pi/3$. Find
the resultant amplitude. At what phase difference would they cancel completely?

**5.** *(10 pts)* Two sources emit 500 Hz sound in phase. Find the three smallest path differences
producing (a) constructive and (b) destructive interference.

## Part C — Beats (10 points)

**6.** *(10 pts)* Two tuning forks produce 5 beats per second; one is 512 Hz. Give both possible
frequencies for the other, and describe an experiment that would decide between them.

## Part D — Standing Waves (40 points)

**7.** *(10 pts)* A 0.650 m string fixed at both ends carries waves at 240 m/s. Find $f_1$ and the
first four harmonics.

**8.** *(10 pts)* A string's third harmonic is 420 Hz. Find its fundamental and its fifth harmonic.

**9.** *(10 pts)* A 1.50 m string has $\mu = 4.00$ g/m under 100 N tension. Find $v$, $\lambda_1$, and
$f_1$.

**10.** *(10 pts)* A guitar string sounds 196 Hz open. Where must a fret be placed to sound 262 Hz?
Express the answer as a fraction of the string length measured from the nut.

**1.** (2 pts) A wave has $f = 200$ Hz and $\lambda = 1.70$ m. Find its speed.

&nbsp;

**2.** (3 pts) A string has $\mu = 5.00$ g/m under 45.0 N tension. Find the wave speed.

&nbsp;

**3.** (2 pts) For $y = (0.0300~\text{m})\sin(5.00x + 40.0t)$, state the amplitude, the wavelength,
and the direction of travel.

&nbsp;

**4.** (3 pts) Two identical waves interfere with a phase difference of $\pi$. What is the resultant
amplitude? Where has the energy gone?

&nbsp;

**5.** (2 pts) Two sources emit 425 Hz in phase. What is the smallest non-zero path difference giving
destructive interference?

&nbsp;

**6.** (2 pts) Two forks at 256 Hz and 260 Hz sound together. State the beat frequency and the pitch
heard.

&nbsp;

**7.** (3 pts) A 0.500 m string fixed at both ends has $v = 300$ m/s. Find $f_1$ and $f_3$.

&nbsp;

**8.** (3 pts) A string vibrates in its third harmonic. How many nodes does it have, counting the
ends? How many antinodes?

&nbsp;

**9.** (2 pts) A pulse reflects from a **fixed** end. Is it inverted? Why must it be?

&nbsp;

**10.** (3 pts) True or false, with one sentence each:

(a) A wave transports matter from one place to another.

(b) Doubling a string's tension doubles the wave speed.

(c) A violin and a flute playing A440 differ in timbre because their harmonic amplitudes differ.

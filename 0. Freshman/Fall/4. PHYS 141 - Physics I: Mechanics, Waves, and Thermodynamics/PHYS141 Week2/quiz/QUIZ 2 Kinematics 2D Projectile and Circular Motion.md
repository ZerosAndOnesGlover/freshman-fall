## 2D Kinematics, Projectile Motion & Circular Motion

**1.** (2 pts) A ball rolls off a horizontal table at 3.0 m/s. The table is 1.2 m high. Which statement is correct?

A) The ball's horizontal velocity decreases as it falls
B) The ball's vertical velocity is zero throughout the fall
C) The time to hit the floor depends only on the height, not the horizontal speed
D) The ball hits the floor at a point directly below where it left the table

---

**2.** (2 pts) A projectile is launched at 45° with speed v₀. A second projectile is launched at 30° with the same speed. Compared to the first, the second projectile has:

A) Greater range and greater max height
B) Greater range and smaller max height
C) Smaller range and smaller max height
D) The same range and smaller max height

---

**3.** (3 pts) A ball is kicked at 18 m/s at 40° above horizontal. Find the maximum height reached.

---

**4.** (2 pts) A projectile's velocity at the highest point of its trajectory is:

A) Zero
B) Equal to its launch speed
C) Equal to its horizontal component of launch velocity
D) Equal to its vertical component of launch velocity

---

**5.** (3 pts) A stone is thrown horizontally at 12 m/s from a cliff. It lands 36 m from the base of the cliff. How tall is the cliff?

---

**6.** (2 pts) In uniform circular motion, which quantity is constant?

A) Velocity
B) Acceleration vector
C) Speed
D) All of the above

---

**7.** (3 pts) A wheel of radius 0.50 m rotates at 6.0 rad/s. Find the centripetal acceleration of a point on the rim.

---

**8.** (2 pts) If the speed of an object in circular motion doubles while the radius stays the same, the centripetal acceleration:

A) Doubles
B) Quadruples
C) Is halved
D) Is unchanged

---

**9.** (3 pts) A car travels at 25 m/s around a circular curve of radius 200 m. Find the centripetal acceleration. If the car also accelerates tangentially at 1.5 m/s², find the magnitude of the total acceleration.

---

**10.** (3 pts) At what launch angle is the horizontal range of a projectile equal to 4 times the maximum height? *(Set R = 4H and solve for θ.)*

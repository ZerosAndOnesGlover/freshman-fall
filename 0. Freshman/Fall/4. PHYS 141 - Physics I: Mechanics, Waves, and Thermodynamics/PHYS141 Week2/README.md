## Learning Objectives

By the end of Week 2, you will be able to:

1. Express position, velocity, and acceleration as 2D vectors and differentiate/integrate component-wise
2. State and apply the principle of independence of perpendicular motion components
3. Derive the projectile motion equations from the 1D kinematic equations applied independently to x and y
4. Solve projectile problems: range, maximum height, time of flight, launch angle for maximum range
5. Derive and apply the formula for centripetal acceleration a = v²/r = ω²r
6. Distinguish speed, velocity, and acceleration in uniform circular motion
7. Convert between angular quantities (θ, ω, α) and their linear counterparts (s, v, a_t)
8. Recognize when an object undergoes non-uniform circular motion and identify both centripetal and tangential acceleration components

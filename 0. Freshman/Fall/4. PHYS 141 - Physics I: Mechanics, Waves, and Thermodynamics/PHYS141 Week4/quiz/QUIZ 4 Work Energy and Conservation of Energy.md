**1.** (2 pts) A force of 40 N acts at 60° to the direction of a 5.0 m displacement. Find the work done.

---

**2.** (2 pts) Which force does zero work on an object moving in a circle at constant speed?

A) Gravity
B) Applied tangential force
C) Centripetal force
D) Friction

---

**3.** (3 pts) A 4.0 kg object accelerates from 3.0 m/s to 7.0 m/s. Find the work done on it (use the work-energy theorem).

---

**4.** (2 pts) A conservative force is one for which:

A) The work done is always positive
B) The work done is independent of the path taken between two points
C) The force is always constant in magnitude
D) No potential energy can be defined

---

**5.** (3 pts) A spring with k = 400 N/m is compressed 0.10 m. Find the elastic potential energy stored.

---

**6.** (2 pts) An object has potential energy U(x) = x² − 4x (Joules). At what value of x is the object in equilibrium?

---

**7.** (3 pts) A 3.0 kg ball is dropped from rest at height 8.0 m. Using energy conservation, find its speed just before hitting the ground.

---

**8.** (2 pts) A block slides down a frictionless curved ramp and a straight incline of the same height. Which reaches the bottom with greater speed?

A) The curved ramp
B) The straight incline
C) Both reach the same speed
D) Cannot be determined without more information

---

**9.** (3 pts) A 60 kg person climbs a 3.0 m ladder at constant speed in 5.0 s. Find the average power output (against gravity only).

---

**10.** (3 pts) A 2.0 kg block slides 5.0 m across a floor with friction, starting at 6.0 m/s and ending at 4.0 m/s. Find the work done by friction and the coefficient of kinetic friction.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of Week 4, you will be able to:

1. Define work as the dot product of force and displacement, and compute it for constant and varying forces
2. Derive the work-energy theorem from Newton's second law using calculus
3. Distinguish conservative from non-conservative forces and explain the physical basis for this distinction
4. Derive gravitational and spring potential energy from their respective conservative forces
5. State and apply the principle of conservation of mechanical energy
6. Solve problems involving conversion between kinetic, gravitational, and elastic potential energy
7. Account for energy dissipated by friction in the full energy conservation equation
8. Define and compute power as the rate of energy transfer

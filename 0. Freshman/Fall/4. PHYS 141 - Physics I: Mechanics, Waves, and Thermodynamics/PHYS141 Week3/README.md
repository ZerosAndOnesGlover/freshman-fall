## Learning Objectives

By the end of Week 3, you will be able to:

1. State Newton's three laws precisely and identify when each applies
2. Distinguish mass (inertial) from weight (gravitational force)
3. Draw a correct free body diagram for any object in any situation
4. Apply Newton's second law (ΣF = ma) component-wise to solve for unknown forces or accelerations
5. Identify Newton's third law pairs correctly and explain why they do NOT cancel
6. Solve problems involving normal force, tension, static and kinetic friction, and inclined planes
7. Analyze connected-body systems (Atwood machine, blocks on surfaces, pulleys)
8. Explain the difference between static and kinetic friction and apply both coefficients correctly

# PHYS 141 · Quiz 3
## Newton's Three Laws of Motion

**Administered:** Monday 19 October 2026, 14:00 (start of lecture) · Week 4, the Monday after this material is covered — covers Week 3.
**Time limit:** 20 minutes | **Format:** Closed book
**Formula sheet:** g = 9.81 m/s²; F = ma; f_k = μ_k N; f_s ≤ μ_s N; Atwood: a = (m₂−m₁)g/(m₁+m₂)

---

**1.** (2 pts) A net force of 30 N acts on a 6.0 kg object initially at rest. What is the object's speed after 4.0 s?

---

**2.** (2 pts) Which of the following is the correct Newton's Third Law pair to "the Earth pulls the Moon gravitationally"?

A) The Moon pulls the Earth gravitationally
B) The Earth's surface pushes up on objects resting on it
C) The Moon orbits the Earth in a circle
D) The centripetal force on the Moon points toward Earth

---

**3.** (3 pts) A 12 kg box is pulled across a horizontal floor by a horizontal force of 60 N. The coefficient of kinetic friction is 0.30. Find the acceleration of the box.

---

**4.** (2 pts) A block sits on a frictionless incline at 40°. What is its acceleration?

A) 9.81 m/s²
B) 9.81 cos40° m/s²
C) 9.81 sin40° m/s²
D) 0 m/s²

---

**5.** (3 pts) A 5.0 kg block hangs from a rope attached to the ceiling of an elevator. The tension in the rope is 60 N. What is the magnitude and direction of the elevator's acceleration?

---

**6.** (2 pts) Static friction on a stationary object is:

A) Always equal to μ_s N
B) Always less than μ_s N
C) Equal to the applied force, up to a maximum of μ_s N
D) Independent of the applied force

---

**7.** (3 pts) An Atwood machine has m₁ = 2.0 kg and m₂ = 6.0 kg. Find the acceleration and the tension in the string.

---

**8.** (2 pts) A 4.0 kg block on a frictionless surface is connected by a string over a pulley to a 1.0 kg hanging mass. What is the acceleration of the system?

---

**9.** (3 pts) A 3.0 kg block is on a 30° frictionless incline, connected over a pulley to a 2.0 kg hanging mass. Which way does the system accelerate? Find the acceleration.

---

**10.** (3 pts) You push a 20 kg crate at constant velocity across a floor with a force of 70 N directed 20° below horizontal. Find the coefficient of kinetic friction between the crate and the floor.

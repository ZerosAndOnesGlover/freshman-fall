**1.** (2 pts) A net force of 30 N acts on a 6.0 kg object initially at rest. What is the object's speed after 4.0 s?

**2.** (2 pts) Which of the following is the correct Newton's Third Law pair to "the Earth pulls the Moon gravitationally"?

**3.** (3 pts) A 12 kg box is pulled across a horizontal floor by a horizontal force of 60 N. The coefficient of kinetic friction is 0.30. Find the acceleration of the box.

**4.** (2 pts) A block sits on a frictionless incline at 40°. What is its acceleration?

**5.** (3 pts) A 5.0 kg block hangs from a rope attached to the ceiling of an elevator. The tension in the rope is 60 N. What is the magnitude and direction of the elevator's acceleration?

### Answer Key (Instructor Copy — Do Not Distribute)

**1.** a = F/m = 30/6.0 = 5.0 m/s². v = at = 5.0×4.0 = **20 m/s**

**2.** A — "The Moon pulls the Earth gravitationally" is the exact Third Law partner: same type of force (gravity), reversed objects.

**3.** N = mg = 12×9.81 = 117.7 N. f_k = 0.30×117.7 = 35.3 N.
ΣFₓ = 60−35.3 = 24.7 N = 12·a → **a = 2.06 m/s²**

**4.** C — a = g sinθ for frictionless incline.

**5.** Weight = mg = 5×9.81 = 49.05 N. T = 60 N > mg → elevator accelerates upward.
ΣF = T − mg = ma → 60−49.05 = 5.0·a → a = 10.95/5.0 = **2.19 m/s² upward**

**6.** C

**7.** a = (6−2)×9.81/(6+2) = 39.24/8 = **4.91 m/s²**
T = 2m₁m₂g/(m₁+m₂) = 2(2)(6)(9.81)/8 = 235.4/8 = **29.4 N**

**8.** Net force = m₂g = 1.0×9.81 = 9.81 N. Total mass = 5.0 kg.
a = 9.81/5.0 = **1.96 m/s²**

**9.** Force pulling block up incline = m₂g = 2.0×9.81 = 19.6 N.
Component of block's weight along incline (downward) = m₁g sin30° = 3.0×9.81×0.5 = 14.7 N.
19.6 > 14.7 → **hanging mass pulls block up** the incline.

Net force = 19.6 − 14.7 = 4.9 N. Total mass = 5.0 kg.
**a = 4.9/5.0 = 0.98 m/s²** (hanging mass down, block up the slope)

**10.** Constant velocity → a = 0. Force applied at 20° below horizontal:
Fₓ = 70cos20° = 65.8 N (forward). Fᵧ = −70sin20° = −23.9 N (downward, adding to weight).
ΣFᵧ = 0: N − mg − Fsin20° = 0 → N = 20×9.81 + 23.9 = 196.2 + 23.9 = 220.1 N
ΣFₓ = 0 (constant v): Fcos20° − f_k = 0 → f_k = 65.8 N
μ_k = f_k/N = 65.8/220.1 = **0.299 ≈ 0.30**

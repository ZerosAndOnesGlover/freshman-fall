# PHYS 141 · Quiz 11
## Temperature, Heat, and Thermal Expansion

**Administered:** Monday of Week 12, the Monday after this material is covered — covers Week 11.
**Time limit:** 20 minutes | **Format:** Closed book
**Formula sheet:** $T_K = T_C+273.15$; $\Delta L = \alpha L_0\Delta T$; $\beta = 3\alpha$;
$Q = mc\Delta T$; $Q = mL$; $P = kA\Delta T/L$; $P = e\sigma AT^4$; $PV = nRT$;
$c_{\text{water}} = 4186$ J·kg⁻¹K⁻¹; $L_f = 3.34\times10^5$ J/kg;
$\sigma = 5.67\times10^{-8}$; $R = 8.314$

---

**1.** (2 pts) Convert $25.0°$C to kelvin and to Fahrenheit.

&nbsp;

**2.** (3 pts) A steel rod ($\alpha = 1.2\times10^{-5}$ K⁻¹) is 3.00 m long at $20.0°$C. Find its
length at $70.0°$C.

&nbsp;

**3.** (2 pts) A metal plate with a circular hole is heated. Does the hole get larger or smaller?
Justify in one sentence.

&nbsp;

**4.** (3 pts) How much energy raises 1.50 kg of water by $40.0$ K?

&nbsp;

**5.** (3 pts) How much energy melts 0.200 kg of ice at $0°$C? Why does the temperature not change
during this process?

&nbsp;

**6.** (3 pts) A 0.100 kg aluminium block ($c = 900$) at $80.0°$C is placed in 0.200 kg of water at
$20.0°$C. Write the calorimetry equation. You need not solve it.

&nbsp;

**7.** (3 pts) A wall has $k = 0.040$, $A = 10.0$ m², $L = 0.050$ m, and a temperature difference of
15.0 K. Find the conduction rate.

&nbsp;

**8.** (3 pts) An object at 300 K has its absolute temperature doubled. By what factor does its
radiated power increase?

&nbsp;

**9.** (2 pts) A sealed rigid container of gas is heated from 300 K to 450 K. By what factor does the
pressure rise?

&nbsp;

**10.** (2 pts) True or false, with one sentence each:

(a) A hot object contains more heat than a cold one.

(b) At the same temperature, all gas molecules have the same average kinetic energy.

&nbsp;

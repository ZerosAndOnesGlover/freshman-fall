## Part A — Temperature and Expansion (20 points)

**1.** *(10 pts)* A steel railway rail is 25.000 m long at $10.0°$C. Find its length at $45.0°$C.

**2.** *(10 pts)* A steel bridge is 1.20 km long. Find its total expansion between $-10.0°$C and
$40.0°$C, and comment on the engineering consequence.

## Part B — Heat and Calorimetry (40 points)

**3.** *(10 pts)* How much energy raises 2.50 kg of water from $15.0°$C to $85.0°$C?

**4.** *(10 pts)* Find the total energy needed to convert 0.250 kg of ice at $-10.0°$C into steam at
$100°$C. Present your answer as a table of the four stages, and state what fraction is used in
boiling.

**5.** *(10 pts)* A 0.150 kg aluminium block at $150°$C is dropped into 0.400 kg of water at
$18.0°$C. Find the final temperature, and verify that heat lost equals heat gained.

**6.** *(10 pts)* 0.080 kg of ice at $0°$C is added to 0.250 kg of water at $30.0°$C. **First
determine whether all the ice melts**, showing the comparison, then find the final temperature.

## Part C — Heat Transfer (20 points)

**7.** *(10 pts)* A single-glazed window of area 2.00 m² and thickness 4.00 mm has $k = 0.800$.
Find the conduction rate with 20.0°C inside and 0°C outside.

**8.** *(10 pts)* A blackbody sphere of radius 0.100 m is at 500 K. Find its radiated power.

## Part D — The Ideal Gas (20 points)

**9.** *(10 pts)* A gas occupies 0.0500 m³ at $1.50\times10^5$ Pa and 300 K. Find the number of
moles, and the pressure after isothermal compression to 0.0200 m³.

**10.** *(10 pts)* Find the rms speed of a nitrogen molecule ($M = 0.0280$ kg/mol) and of a hydrogen
molecule ($M = 0.00202$ kg/mol), both at 300 K. Comment on the ratio.

**2.** (3 pts) A source emits 1.00 W uniformly. Find the intensity at 2.00 m.

**3.** (2 pts) A sound has intensity $10^{-5}$ W/m². Find its level in dB.

&nbsp;

**4.** (3 pts) By how many dB does the level change if the intensity is doubled? If it is multiplied

&nbsp;

**5.** (2 pts) You move from 3.00 m to 6.00 m from a source. By how many dB does the level fall?

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

harmonic?

&nbsp;

**8.** (3 pts) A 500 Hz source approaches a stationary listener at 20.0 m/s. Find the frequency
heard. State first whether it should be higher or lower.

&nbsp;

**9.** (2 pts) The same source now recedes at 20.0 m/s. Find the frequency heard.

&nbsp;

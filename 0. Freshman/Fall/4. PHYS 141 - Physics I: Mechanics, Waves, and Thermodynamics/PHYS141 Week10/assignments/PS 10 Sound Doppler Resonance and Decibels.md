## Part A — Sound Waves and Intensity (30 points)

**1.** *(10 pts)* A source emits 2.00 W uniformly in all directions. Find the intensity and the sound
level at 3.00 m.

**2.** *(10 pts)* A sound has intensity $4.00\times10^{-6}$ W/m². Find its level in dB.

**3.** *(10 pts)* At 5.00 m from a point source the level is 78.0 dB. Find the level at 20.0 m and the
acoustic power of the source.

## Part B — Decibels and Perception (10 points)

**4.** *(10 pts)* Two identical machines each produce 70.0 dB at your position. What is the level with
both running? Explain why the answer is not 140 dB.

## Part C — Air Columns and Resonance (30 points)

**5.** *(10 pts)* An open–open pipe is 0.750 m long. Find its first three harmonics.

**6.** *(10 pts)* A closed–open pipe is 0.750 m long. Find its first three harmonics and compare with
Problem 5. State the ratio of the two fundamentals.

**7.** *(10 pts)* A resonance tube driven by a 384 Hz fork resonates when the air column is 0.216 m
and again at 0.663 m. Find the wavelength, the speed of sound, and the end correction.

## Part D — The Doppler Effect (30 points)

**8.** *(10 pts)* A train sounds a 440 Hz horn while approaching at 30.0 m/s. Find the frequency
heard by a stationary observer before and after it passes. State the total drop in pitch.

**9.** *(10 pts)* A car approaches a source at 20.0 m/s while the source moves towards the car at
15.0 m/s. The source emits 800 Hz. Find the received frequency.

**10.** *(10 pts)* A bat emits 45.0 kHz and flies at 8.00 m/s directly towards a stationary wall.
Find (a) the frequency arriving at the wall and (b) the frequency the bat hears reflected back.
Explain why **two** Doppler shifts are involved, and compute the beat frequency the bat detects
against its own call.

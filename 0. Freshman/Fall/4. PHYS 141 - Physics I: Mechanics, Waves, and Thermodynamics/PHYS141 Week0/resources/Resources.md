# PHYS 141: Week 0 Resources

## Required Textbook Reading

- **Halliday, Resnick & Krane**, *Physics*, 5th ed. — Chapter 1 (Measurement), Chapter 3 (Vectors)
- **Serway & Jewett**, *Physics for Scientists and Engineers*, 10th ed. — Chapter 1 (Physics and Measurement), Chapter 3 (Vectors)

## Supplementary / Enrichment Reading

- NIST SI Brochure (free, online): the official definitions of all 7 SI base units post-2019 redefinition
- Richard Feynman, *Six Easy Pieces* — Chapter 1, "Atoms in Motion" (excellent on orders of magnitude and the atomic scale)
- 3Blue1Brown, "Essence of Linear Algebra" video series — Episodes 1–2 cover vectors with outstanding visual intuition (not required, highly recommended)

## Simulations & Interactive Tools

- PhET Interactive Simulations (University of Colorado Boulder) — "Vector Addition" simulation: practice tip-to-tail and component addition interactively
- GeoGebra 3D Calculator — useful for visualizing coordinate system conversions

## A Note on Studying Physics

Physics is learned by **doing**, not by reading passively. After each lecture, work through the worked examples on your own (cover the solution, attempt it, then check). Treat the conceptual questions at the end of each lecture as seriously as the graded problem sets — they build the intuition that graded problems test.

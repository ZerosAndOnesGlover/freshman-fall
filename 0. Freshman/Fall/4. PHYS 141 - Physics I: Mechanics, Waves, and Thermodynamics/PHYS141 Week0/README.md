## Learning Objectives

By the end of Week 0, you will be able to:

1. State the seven SI base units and derive any SI derived unit from them
2. Apply dimensional analysis to check equations and derive unknown relationships
3. Perform calculations with correct significant figures and scientific notation
4. Convert between coordinate systems (Cartesian ↔ polar ↔ spherical)
5. Add, subtract, scale, and decompose vectors algebraically and geometrically
6. Compute dot products and cross products and interpret their physical meaning
7. Propagate measurement uncertainties through arithmetic operations
8. Estimate orders of magnitude for physical quantities

# PHYS 141 · Week 6 · Summary

**Topic —** Rotational kinematics and dynamics
**Lectures —** L19 Rigid Body Rotation and Kinematics; L20 Torque and Rotational Dynamics; L21 Rotational Energy and Rolling Motion.
**Work —** PS 6, Lab 6 (moment of inertia and rolling), Quiz 6, **Midterm Exam** (90 min, Weeks 0–6, 15%).
**Takeaway —** Rotation is translation with every symbol replaced: m becomes I, F becomes τ, v becomes ω. The moment of inertia depends on the axis, which is why a hoop and a disc roll down at different rates.

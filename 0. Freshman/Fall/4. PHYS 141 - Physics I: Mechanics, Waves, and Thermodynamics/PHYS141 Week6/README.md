## Learning Objectives

By the end of Week 6, you will be able to:

1. Define the rigid body model and describe rotational motion using angular position, velocity, and acceleration
2. Apply the angular kinematic equations for constant angular acceleration
3. Define torque as a cross product and compute it for forces applied at various points and angles
4. Derive Newton's second law for rotation (τ = Iα) from the tangential form of F = ma
5. Compute the moment of inertia for simple shapes using integration, and apply the parallel axis theorem
6. Define rotational kinetic energy and combine it with translational kinetic energy for rolling objects
7. State and apply the rolling-without-slipping condition
8. Solve energy-conservation problems for objects rolling down inclines, and explain why different shapes reach the bottom at different times

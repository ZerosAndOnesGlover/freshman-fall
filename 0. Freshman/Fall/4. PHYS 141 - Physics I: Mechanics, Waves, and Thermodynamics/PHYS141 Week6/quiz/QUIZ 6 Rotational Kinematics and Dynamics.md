# PHYS 141 — Quiz 6
## Rotational Kinematics & Dynamics

**Time limit:** 20 minutes | **Format:** Closed book
**Formula sheet:** ω=ω₀+αt; Δθ=ω₀t+½αt²; ω²=ω₀²+2αΔθ; τ=Iα; τ=Fd; I_disk=½MR²; I_hoop=MR²; I_sphere=(2/5)MR²; KE_rot=½Iω²; v_cm=Rω (rolling); a=g sinθ/(1+β)

---

**1.** (2 pts) A wheel starts at rest and reaches 20 rad/s in 4.0 s. Find the angular acceleration.

---

**2.** (2 pts) A point on a rotating wheel at radius 0.50 m has angular velocity 8.0 rad/s. Find its tangential speed.

---

**3.** (3 pts) A torque of 15 N·m is applied to a wheel with moment of inertia 3.0 kg·m². Find the angular acceleration.

---

**4.** (2 pts) A force is applied at the exact pivot point of a rotating object (r=0). What is the torque?

A) Equal to the force magnitude
B) Zero, regardless of the force
C) Maximum possible torque
D) Depends on the force direction only

---

**5.** (3 pts) A uniform disk of mass 4.0 kg and radius 0.30 m rotates about its central axis. Find its moment of inertia.

---

**6.** (2 pts) Which object, released from rest at the same height on the same incline, reaches the bottom FASTEST when rolling without slipping?

A) A solid sphere
B) A solid cylinder
C) A thin hoop
D) All arrive at the same time

---

**7.** (3 pts) A solid sphere (M=1.0 kg, R=0.10 m) rolls without slipping at v=2.0 m/s. Find its total kinetic energy (translational + rotational).

---

**8.** (2 pts) The parallel axis theorem states:

A) I = I_cm − Md²
B) I = I_cm + Md²
C) I = I_cm × d²
D) I = I_cm / d²

---

**9.** (3 pts) A wrench applies a 25 N force at a 90° angle (perpendicular) to its 0.20 m handle. Find the torque.

---

**10.** (3 pts) A solid cylinder rolls without slipping down an incline at angle 20°. Find its acceleration. (β = 0.5 for a solid cylinder.)

---

**Total: 25 points**

---

### Answer Key (Instructor Copy — Do Not Distribute)

**1.** α = Δω/t = 20/4.0 = **5.0 rad/s²**

**2.** v = rω = 0.50×8.0 = **4.0 m/s**

**3.** α = τ/I = 15/3.0 = **5.0 rad/s²**

**4. B** — τ=rFsinφ; with r=0, τ=0 regardless of F.

**5.** I = ½MR² = ½(4.0)(0.09) = **0.18 kg·m²**

**6. A** — solid sphere has smallest β=0.4, converting the most PE to translational KE.

**7.** ω=v/R=2.0/0.10=20 rad/s. I=(2/5)(1.0)(0.01)=0.004 kg·m².
KE_trans=½(1.0)(4.0)=2.0 J. KE_rot=½(0.004)(400)=0.8 J.
**Total = 2.8 J** (or use shortcut: ½Mv²(1+β)=½(1.0)(4.0)(1.4)=2.8 J ✓)

**8. B**

**9.** τ = rFsin90° = 0.20×25×1 = **5.0 N·m**

**10.** a = g sin20°/(1+0.5) = 9.81×0.342/1.5 = 3.355/1.5 = **2.24 m/s²**

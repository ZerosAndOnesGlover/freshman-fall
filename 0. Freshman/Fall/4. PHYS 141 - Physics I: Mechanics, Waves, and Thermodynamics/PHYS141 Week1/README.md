## Learning Objectives

By the end of Week 1, you will be able to:

1. Distinguish position, displacement, distance, speed, and velocity — precisely
2. Compute average velocity and average acceleration from data
3. Derive instantaneous velocity and acceleration as derivatives of x(t)
4. Derive all five constant-acceleration kinematic equations from first principles (calculus)
5. Solve 1D kinematics problems — including free fall — using the kinematic equations
6. Choose the correct sign convention and apply it consistently throughout a problem
7. Draw and interpret x(t), v(t), and a(t) graphs — and convert between them geometrically

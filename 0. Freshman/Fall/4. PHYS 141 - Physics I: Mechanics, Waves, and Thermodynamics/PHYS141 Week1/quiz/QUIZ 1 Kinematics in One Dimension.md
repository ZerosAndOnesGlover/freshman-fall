# PHYS 141 · Quiz 1
## Kinematics in One Dimension

**Time limit:** 20 minutes | **Format:** Closed book, formula sheet provided (kinematic equations only)
**Administered:** Monday 5 October 2026, 14:00 (start of lecture) · Week 2

*Formula sheet provided:*
v = v₀ + at | Δx = v₀t + ½at² | v² = v₀² + 2aΔx | Δx = ½(v₀+v)t | g = 9.81 m/s²

---

**1.** (2 pts) A particle moves from x = −3 m to x = +5 m. Its displacement is:

A) 2 m  &nbsp;&nbsp; B) 8 m  &nbsp;&nbsp; C) −8 m  &nbsp;&nbsp; D) Cannot be determined without knowing the path

---

**2.** (2 pts) On a position-time graph, the instantaneous velocity at time t is:

A) The area under the curve from 0 to t
B) The slope of the secant line from 0 to t
C) The slope of the tangent line at t
D) The average of the slopes on either side of t

---

**3.** (3 pts) A car has position x(t) = 2t² − 8t + 3 (meters, t in seconds). Find the instantaneous velocity at t = 3 s.

---

**4.** (2 pts) An object moves with v = +5 m/s and has acceleration a = −2 m/s². Which statement is correct?

A) The object is moving in the negative direction
B) The object is slowing down
C) The object is speeding up
D) The object is at rest

---

**5.** (3 pts) A car accelerates from rest to 24 m/s over a distance of 72 m. Find the acceleration (assume constant).

---

**6.** (2 pts) Which kinematic equation would you use to find final velocity given initial velocity, acceleration, and displacement — but NOT time?

A) v = v₀ + at
B) Δx = v₀t + ½at²
C) v² = v₀² + 2aΔx
D) Δx = ½(v₀+v)t

---

**7.** (3 pts) A ball is thrown straight up with v₀ = 14 m/s. How high does it rise above the launch point? (Take g = 9.81 m/s², upward positive.)

---

**8.** (2 pts) A ball is dropped from rest and falls for time t. A second ball is dropped from the same point 1 s later. As time goes on, the distance between the two balls:

A) Remains constant
B) Decreases
C) Increases at a constant rate
D) Increases at an increasing rate

---

**9.** (3 pts) A v(t) graph is a straight line passing through the origin with slope 4 m/s². The object starts at x = 0.

(a) What is the acceleration?
(b) Write the equation for x(t).
(c) What is the displacement from t = 0 to t = 3 s?

---

**10.** (3 pts) An object is thrown downward from a height of 25 m with initial speed 5.0 m/s (taking downward as positive, origin at the throw point, a = +9.81 m/s²). How long does it take to reach the ground?

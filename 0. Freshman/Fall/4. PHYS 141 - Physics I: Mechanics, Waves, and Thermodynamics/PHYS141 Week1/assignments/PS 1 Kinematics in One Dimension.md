### Part A: Displacement, Velocity & the Derivative (Problems 1–3) — 30 pts

**1.** A particle's position is described by x(t) = 4t³ − 9t² + 3 (meters, t in seconds).

(a) Find the position at t = 0, t = 1 s, and t = 3 s.
(b) Find the average velocity between t = 0 and t = 3 s.
(c) Find the instantaneous velocity v(t) by taking the derivative.
(d) At what time(s) is the particle momentarily at rest?
(e) Find the instantaneous acceleration a(t). Is this a constant-acceleration problem?

---

**2.** A jogger runs 400 m due east in 80 s, then turns around and runs 150 m due west in 40 s.

(a) What is the jogger's total displacement?
(b) What is the total distance traveled?
(c) What is the average velocity for the entire trip?
(d) What is the average speed for the entire trip?
(e) If the jogger's eastward speed was constant at 5.0 m/s, and the westward speed was constant at 3.75 m/s, sketch a qualitative x(t) graph for the entire trip.

---

**3.** The velocity of an object is given by v(t) = 6t² − 4t (m/s), with the object at x = 2 m at t = 0.

(a) Find the acceleration as a function of time.
(b) Find the position as a function of time by integration.
(c) Find the displacement between t = 0 and t = 3 s using the integral of v(t). Verify your answer using x(t).
(d) Is the acceleration constant? What does this mean for using the 5 kinematic equations?

### Part B: Constant Acceleration & Kinematic Equations (Problems 4–6) — 30 pts

**4.** A car initially moving at 20 m/s decelerates uniformly and stops in 4.0 s.

(a) Find the acceleration.
(b) Find the stopping distance.
(c) Solve again using a different kinematic equation from (b) to verify.

---

**5.** Two cars are racing on a straight track. Car A starts from rest with constant acceleration a_A = 4.0 m/s². Car B starts at the same time but from a position 50 m ahead of A, with constant velocity v_B = 15 m/s.

(a) Write position equations x_A(t) and x_B(t) for both cars.
(b) At what time does Car A catch Car B?
(c) How fast is Car A going when it catches Car B?
(d) Sketch x(t) for both cars on the same graph, labeling the catch-up point.

---

**6. (Quadratic reasoning)** A particle starts at x = 0 with v₀ = +10 m/s and has constant acceleration a = −2.0 m/s².

(a) Find all times when the particle is at x = +12 m. Interpret both solutions physically.
(b) Find the time when the particle returns to x = 0. What is its velocity at that point?
(c) What is the farthest positive x the particle reaches?
(d) At what time is the particle farthest in the negative x direction? (Think carefully about this — does the particle keep accelerating in the negative direction indefinitely given this acceleration?)

---

### Part C: Free Fall (Problems 7–10) — 40 pts

**7.** A stone is dropped (v₀ = 0) from a cliff of unknown height. It strikes the ground 3.5 s later.

(a) How tall is the cliff?
(b) What is the stone's speed on impact?
(c) Sketch x(t) and v(t) graphs for the fall.

---

**8.** A ball is thrown straight upward with v₀ = 18 m/s from ground level. Take upward as positive.

(a) Find the maximum height reached.
(b) Find the time to reach maximum height.
(c) Find the total time in the air (until it returns to ground level).
(d) Find the velocity at t = 1.5 s and t = 3.2 s. Interpret the sign of each.
(e) At t = 2.5 s, is the ball moving upward or downward? How do you know without computing the position?

---

**9.** A ball is thrown upward from the edge of a building 30 m tall with v₀ = +12 m/s (taking up as positive, origin at the top of the building).

(a) Find the maximum height above the building top.
(b) Find the time when the ball hits the ground (30 m below the launch point).
(c) Find the speed on impact.
(d) Find the total distance traveled by the ball (not displacement — actual path length).

---

**10. (Synthesis)** A ball is dropped from height H. At the same instant, a second ball is thrown upward from the ground with speed v₀.

(a) At what time t do the two balls meet?
(b) At what height do they meet?
(c) What condition on v₀ is necessary for the balls to meet before the first ball hits the ground?
(d) If H = 20 m, what minimum v₀ is needed for the balls to meet?

*(Express your answers algebraically first, then substitute numbers for parts (c) and (d).)*

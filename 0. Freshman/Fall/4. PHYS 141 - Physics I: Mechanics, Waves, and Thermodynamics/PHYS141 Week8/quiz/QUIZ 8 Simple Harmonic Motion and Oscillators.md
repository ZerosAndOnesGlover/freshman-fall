# PHYS 141 · Quiz 8
## Simple Harmonic Motion and Oscillators

**Administered:** Monday of Week 9, the Monday after this material is covered — covers Week 8.
**Time limit:** 20 minutes | **Format:** Closed book
**Formula sheet:** $\omega=\sqrt{k/m}$; $T=2\pi/\omega$; $x=A\cos(\omega t+\phi)$;
$E=\tfrac12kA^2$; $v=\omega\sqrt{A^2-x^2}$; $T_{\text{pend}}=2\pi\sqrt{L/g}$;
$T_{\text{phys}}=2\pi\sqrt{I/mgd}$; $\gamma=b/2m$; $g=9.81$ m/s²

---

**1.** (2 pts) A 0.200 kg mass on a spring of $k = 8.00$ N/m oscillates. Find the period.

&nbsp;

**2.** (2 pts) An oscillator has $A = 5.00$ cm and $\omega = 10.0$ rad/s. Find $v_{\max}$ and
$a_{\max}$.

&nbsp;

**3.** (3 pts) An oscillator of amplitude $A$ is at $x = A/2$. What fraction of its total energy is
kinetic?

&nbsp;

**4.** (2 pts) A simple pendulum has length 0.500 m. Find its period.

&nbsp;

**5.** (2 pts) The amplitude of a pendulum is doubled (staying below $10°$). By what factor does the
period change?

&nbsp;

**6.** (3 pts) A uniform rod of length $L$ pivoted at one end has $I = \tfrac13mL^2$. Show that its
period is $2\pi\sqrt{2L/3g}$.

&nbsp;

**7.** (3 pts) A damped oscillator has $m = 0.500$ kg, $k = 50.0$ N/m, $b = 0.400$ kg/s. Is it under-,
critically, or overdamped? Show the comparison.

&nbsp;

**8.** (3 pts) For that oscillator, find the time for the amplitude to fall to half its initial value.

&nbsp;

**9.** (2 pts) A driven oscillator has $\omega_0 = 25.0$ rad/s. At which driving frequency is the
steady-state amplitude greatest, and what is this phenomenon called?

&nbsp;

**10.** (3 pts) True or false, with one sentence of justification each:

(a) The period of a mass–spring system depends on the amplitude.

(b) The period of a simple pendulum depends on the mass of the bob.

(c) In a damped oscillator, the energy decays twice as fast as the amplitude.

&nbsp;

## Part A — SHM Kinematics (30 points)

**1.** *(10 pts)* A 0.750 kg mass on a spring of $k = 30.0$ N/m is displaced 8.00 cm from equilibrium
and released from rest. Find $\omega$, $T$, $f$, $v_{\max}$, $a_{\max}$, and the total energy.

**2.** *(10 pts)* An SHM oscillator obeys $x(t) = (0.250~\text{m})\cos(12.0t + \pi/3)$. State $A$,
$\omega$, $T$, $f$, $\phi$, and $x(0)$. Find the first time after $t=0$ at which the mass passes
through $x = 0$.

**3.** *(10 pts)* For an oscillator of amplitude $A$, find the displacement at which the speed is
exactly half its maximum value. Express the answer as a multiple of $A$.

## Part B — Energy in SHM (20 points)

**4.** *(10 pts)* For the oscillator of Problem 1, find the kinetic and potential energies at
$x = 4.00$ cm, and verify they sum to the total.

**5.** *(10 pts)* At what displacement (as a fraction of $A$) is the kinetic energy exactly equal to
the potential energy?

## Part C — Pendulums (20 points)

**6.** *(10 pts)* Find the length of a simple pendulum whose period is exactly 1.00 s on Earth.

**7.** *(10 pts)* A uniform rod of length 0.800 m swings from one end. Find its period and its
equivalent simple-pendulum length. Explain why it swings faster than a simple pendulum of the same
length.

## Part D — Damping and Resonance (30 points)

**8.** *(10 pts)* An oscillator has $m = 0.250$ kg, $k = 100$ N/m, and $b = 0.500$ kg/s. Find
$\omega_0$, $\gamma$, $\omega_d$, and $Q$. Classify the damping regime, showing the comparison you
used.

**9.** *(10 pts)* For the oscillator of Problem 8, how long until the amplitude falls to 25% of its
initial value? How long until the **energy** does? Explain why the two answers differ by a factor of
two.

**10.** *(10 pts)* A driven oscillator has $\omega_0 = 40.0$ rad/s, $\gamma = 1.50$ s⁻¹, and
$F_0/m = 2.00$ m/s². Compute the steady-state amplitude at $\omega = 20.0$, $38.0$, $40.0$, and
$42.0$ rad/s. Identify the resonance and comment on the sharpness of the peak.

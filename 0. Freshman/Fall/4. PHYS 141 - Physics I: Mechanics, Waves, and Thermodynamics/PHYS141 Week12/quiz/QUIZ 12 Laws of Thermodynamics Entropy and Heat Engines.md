# PHYS 141 — Quiz 12
## The Laws of Thermodynamics, Entropy, and Heat Engines

**Time limit:** 20 minutes | **Format:** Closed book
**Formula sheet:** $\Delta U = Q - W$; $W = \int P\,dV$; isothermal $W = nRT\ln(V_2/V_1)$;
$e = 1 - Q_c/Q_h$; $e_{\text{Carnot}} = 1 - T_c/T_h$; $\text{COP}_{\text{fr}} = T_c/(T_h-T_c)$;
$\Delta S = Q/T$; $R = 8.314$; $L_f = 3.34\times10^5$ J/kg

---

**1.** (2 pts) A gas absorbs 600 J and does 250 J of work. Find $\Delta U$.

&nbsp;

**2.** (3 pts) One mole of ideal gas expands isothermally at 400 K, doubling its volume. Find $W$,
$\Delta U$, and $Q$.

&nbsp;

**3.** (2 pts) A gas is heated at constant **volume**. How much work does it do? Explain in one
sentence.

&nbsp;

**4.** (3 pts) A gas expands adiabatically. Does its temperature rise or fall? Justify using the
First Law.

&nbsp;

**5.** (3 pts) An engine absorbs 900 J and rejects 600 J per cycle. Find $W$ and $e$.

&nbsp;

**6.** (3 pts) Find the Carnot efficiency for an engine between 800 K and 320 K.

&nbsp;

**7.** (2 pts) An engine claims 70% efficiency between 500 K and 250 K. Is this possible?

&nbsp;

**8.** (3 pts) A refrigerator operates between 270 K and 300 K. Find its maximum COP.

&nbsp;

**9.** (3 pts) Find the entropy change when 0.200 kg of ice melts at 273 K.

&nbsp;

**10.** (2 pts) True or false, with one sentence each:

(a) A heat engine can be 100% efficient if built well enough.

(b) The entropy of a system can decrease.

&nbsp;

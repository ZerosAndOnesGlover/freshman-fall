## Part A — The First Law (30 points)

**1.** *(10 pts)* A gas absorbs 800 J of heat and does 300 J of work. Find $\Delta U$.

**2.** *(10 pts)* Two moles of ideal gas expand isothermally at 350 K from 0.0150 m³ to 0.0450 m³.
Find $W$, $\Delta U$, and $Q$.

**3.** *(10 pts)* A gas at a constant $2.00\times10^5$ Pa expands from 0.0200 m³ to 0.0500 m³. Find
the work done.

## Part B — Processes and Cycles (20 points)

**4.** *(10 pts)* A diatomic gas ($\gamma = 1.40$) at $3.00\times10^5$ Pa and 320 K expands
adiabatically from 0.0080 m³ to 0.0240 m³. Find $P_2$, $T_2$, $W$, and $\Delta U$. Explain why the
gas cools despite exchanging no heat.

**5.** *(10 pts)* A gas undergoes a cycle absorbing 1200 J and rejecting 900 J. Find the net work per
cycle and state the direction of travel on a $PV$ diagram.

## Part C — Heat Engines and the Second Law (30 points)

**6.** *(10 pts)* An engine absorbs 2500 J and rejects 1800 J per cycle. Find $W$ and $e$.

**7.** *(10 pts)* A Carnot engine operates between 650 K and 310 K. Find its maximum efficiency.

**8.** *(10 pts)* A refrigerator maintains $4.00°$C in a $22.0°$C kitchen. Find the maximum COP and
the minimum work required to remove 5.00 kJ from the interior.

## Part D — Entropy (20 points)

**9.** *(10 pts)* Find the entropy change when 0.500 kg of ice melts at $0°$C.

**10.** *(10 pts)* 2000 J of heat flows from a 600 K reservoir to a 350 K reservoir. Find $\Delta S$
for each reservoir and the total. Is the process permitted? What would the total be for the reverse
process, and what does that tell you?

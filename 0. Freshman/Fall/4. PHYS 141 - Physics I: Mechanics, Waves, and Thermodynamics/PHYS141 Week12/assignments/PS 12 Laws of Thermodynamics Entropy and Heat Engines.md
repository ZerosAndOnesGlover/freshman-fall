## The Laws of Thermodynamics, Entropy, and Heat Engines

## Part A — The First Law (25 points)

**1.** *(4 pts)* A gas absorbs 800 J of heat and does 300 J of work. Find $\Delta U$.

**2.** *(4 pts)* A gas is compressed, 500 J of work being done **on** it, while it releases 200 J of
heat. Find $\Delta U$. State your sign convention explicitly.

**3.** *(5 pts)* Two moles of ideal gas expand isothermally at 350 K from 0.0150 m³ to 0.0450 m³.
Find $W$, $\Delta U$, and $Q$.

**4.** *(4 pts)* A gas at a constant $2.00\times10^5$ Pa expands from 0.0200 m³ to 0.0500 m³. Find
the work done.

**5.** *(4 pts)* A monatomic ideal gas ($n = 1.50$ mol) is heated at constant volume from 280 K to
400 K. Find $\Delta U$, $W$, and $Q$.

**6.** *(4 pts)* Explain why $U$ is a state function while $Q$ and $W$ are not, and give a concrete
consequence of the difference.

---

## Part B — Processes and Cycles (20 points)

**7.** *(7 pts)* A diatomic gas ($\gamma = 1.40$) at $3.00\times10^5$ Pa and 320 K expands
adiabatically from 0.0080 m³ to 0.0240 m³. Find $P_2$, $T_2$, $W$, and $\Delta U$. Explain why the
gas cools despite exchanging no heat.

**8.** *(5 pts)* A gas undergoes a cycle absorbing 1200 J and rejecting 900 J. Find the net work per
cycle and state the direction of travel on a $PV$ diagram.

**9.** *(4 pts)* Sketch isothermal and adiabatic expansions between the same two volumes on one $PV$
diagram. Which encloses more area, and what does that mean physically?

**10.** *(4 pts)* Explain why the *path* between two states matters for $Q$ and $W$ but not for
$\Delta U$, referring to your sketch in Problem 9.

---

## Part C — Heat Engines and the Second Law (30 points)

**11.** *(4 pts)* An engine absorbs 2500 J and rejects 1800 J per cycle. Find $W$ and $e$.

**12.** *(4 pts)* A Carnot engine operates between 650 K and 310 K. Find its maximum efficiency.

**13.** *(5 pts)* An inventor claims 55% efficiency for an engine between 700 K and 350 K. Evaluate
the claim, showing the comparison that settles it.

**14.** *(4 pts)* A Carnot engine has $e = 0.400$ with $T_c = 290$ K. Find $T_h$.

**15.** *(6 pts)* A refrigerator maintains $4.00°$C in a $22.0°$C kitchen. Find the maximum COP and
the minimum work required to remove 5.00 kJ from the interior.

**16.** *(4 pts)* A heat pump delivers 12.0 kJ to a house using 3.00 kJ of electricity. Find its COP
and compare with a resistive heater. Explain why a COP above 1 does not violate energy conservation.

**17.** *(3 pts)* State both the Clausius and Kelvin–Planck forms of the Second Law, and explain in
one sentence why every heat engine requires a cold reservoir.

---

## Part D — Entropy (25 points)

**18.** *(5 pts)* Find the entropy change when 0.500 kg of ice melts at $0°$C.

**19.** *(7 pts)* 2000 J of heat flows from a 600 K reservoir to a 350 K reservoir. Find $\Delta S$
for each reservoir and the total. Is the process permitted? What would the total be for the reverse
process, and what does that tell you?

**20.** *(5 pts)* Two moles of ideal gas expand isothermally at 300 K from 0.0100 m³ to 0.0300 m³.
Find $\Delta S$ for the gas, using both $Q/T$ and $nR\ln(V_2/V_1)$, and confirm they agree.

**21.** *(4 pts)* Explain what $S = k_B\ln\Omega$ means, and why the Second Law is described as
statistical rather than absolute.

**22.** *(4 pts)* The First Law permits a cup of coffee to spontaneously heat while the room cools.
Compute the sign of $\Delta S_{\text{total}}$ for such a process and use it to explain why it is never
observed.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of Week 5, you will be able to:

1. Define linear momentum and derive the impulse-momentum theorem from Newton's second law
2. Compute impulse from a constant or time-varying force
3. State and apply conservation of momentum for isolated systems
4. Distinguish elastic, inelastic, and perfectly inelastic collisions and solve for unknowns in each case
5. Solve 1D and 2D collision problems using conservation of momentum (and energy, where applicable)
6. Define the center of mass for discrete and continuous mass distributions
7. Show that the center of mass of an isolated system moves at constant velocity, even during internal collisions or explosions
8. Apply the center-of-mass frame to simplify collision analysis

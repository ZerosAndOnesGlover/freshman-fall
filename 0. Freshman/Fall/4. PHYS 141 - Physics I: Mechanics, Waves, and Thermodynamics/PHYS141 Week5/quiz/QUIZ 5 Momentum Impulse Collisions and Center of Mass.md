# PHYS 141 — Quiz 5
## Momentum, Impulse, Collisions & Center of Mass

**Time limit:** 20 minutes | **Format:** Closed book
**Formula sheet:** p=mv; J=FΔt=Δp; perfectly inelastic: v_f=(m₁v₁+m₂v₂)/(m₁+m₂); elastic: v_{1f}=[(m1-m2)/(m1+m2)]v1i+[2m2/(m1+m2)]v2i (and symmetric for v_{2f}); r_cm=Σmr/M

---

**1.** (2 pts) A 2.0 kg object moving at 4.0 m/s has momentum:

A) 2.0 kg·m/s  &nbsp; B) 4.0 kg·m/s  &nbsp; C) 8.0 kg·m/s  &nbsp; D) 16 kg·m/s

---

**2.** (3 pts) A 0.15 kg ball changes velocity from +12 m/s to −8 m/s during a 3.0 ms collision with a bat. Find the average force exerted on the ball.

---

**3.** (2 pts) Which of the following is ALWAYS conserved in a collision within an isolated system?

A) Kinetic energy
B) Momentum
C) Both kinetic energy and momentum
D) Neither, in general

---

**4.** (3 pts) A 4.0 kg cart moving at 3.0 m/s collides with a stationary 2.0 kg cart, and they stick together. Find the common final velocity.

---

**5.** (2 pts) In an elastic collision between two equal masses, with the target initially at rest, what happens after the collision?

A) Both move off together at half the initial speed
B) The incoming object stops; the target moves off at the incoming object's original speed
C) Both objects bounce back
D) The incoming object continues unaffected

---

**6.** (3 pts) A 1.5 kg ball moving at 6.0 m/s collides elastically with a stationary 4.5 kg ball. Find the final velocity of the 1.5 kg ball. (Use the elastic collision formula.)

---

**7.** (2 pts) The center of mass of a system of particles moves according to:

A) The net internal force divided by total mass
B) The net external force divided by total mass
C) The average velocity of all particles, unweighted
D) It never moves regardless of forces

---

**8.** (3 pts) Two point masses: 3.0 kg at x=0, and 5.0 kg at x=8.0 m. Find the x-coordinate of the center of mass.

---

**9.** (2 pts) A firework explodes in mid-air with no external horizontal forces. Before the explosion, its horizontal momentum was 20 kg·m/s. Immediately after explosion, what is the sum of all fragments' horizontal momenta?

---

**10.** (3 pts) A 0.50 kg object falls and hits the ground at 6.0 m/s, then bounces back up at 4.0 m/s. If the bounce lasts 5.0 ms, find the average force exerted by the ground on the object (magnitude only; ignore gravity's contribution during the brief bounce).

---

**Total: 25 points**

---

### Answer Key (Instructor Copy — Do Not Distribute)

**1. C** (p = mv = 2.0×4.0 = 8.0 kg·m/s)

**2.** Δp = 0.15(−8−12) = 0.15(−20) = −3.0 kg·m/s. F̄ = Δp/Δt = −3.0/0.003 = **−1000 N** (magnitude 1000 N)

**3. B** — momentum is always conserved in an isolated system; KE only in elastic collisions.

**4.** v_f = [4.0(3.0)+2.0(0)]/(4.0+2.0) = 12.0/6.0 = **2.0 m/s**

**5. B**

**6.** v_{1f} = [(1.5−4.5)/(1.5+4.5)](6.0) + [2(4.5)/(6.0)](0) = (−3.0/6.0)(6.0) = **−3.0 m/s** (bounces back)

**7. B**

**8.** x_cm = [3.0(0)+5.0(8.0)]/(3.0+5.0) = 40.0/8.0 = **5.0 m**

**9.** By conservation of momentum (explosion = internal forces only), total momentum is unchanged: **20 kg·m/s**

**10.** Taking up as positive: v_i = −6.0 m/s (downward before), v_f = +4.0 m/s (upward after).
Δp = 0.50(4.0−(−6.0)) = 0.50(10.0) = 5.0 kg·m/s
F̄ = Δp/Δt = 5.0/0.005 = **1000 N**

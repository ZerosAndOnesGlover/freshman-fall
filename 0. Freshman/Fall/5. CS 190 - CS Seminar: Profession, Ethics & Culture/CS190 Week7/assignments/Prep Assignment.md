## Part B: Patent, Copyright, or Trade Secret?

For each of the following three hypothetical scenarios, state which IP protection regime(s) — copyright, patent, trade secret, more than one, or none — would apply, and briefly (1–2 sentences each) explain why:

1. A company writes a novel, specific 200-line implementation of a well-known sorting algorithm, with original comments and variable naming choices.
2. A team invents a genuinely new technical method for compressing video data that reduces file size by 40% compared to existing methods, and they want maximum legal protection while being willing to publicly disclose exactly how it works.
3. A company develops an internal method for optimizing its supply chain logistics that gives it a significant cost advantage, and prefers competitors never learn how it works at all.

## Part C: DMCA Tension, Applied

Describe the scenario in 3–4 sentences and state what you think the researcher should do given this legal exposure.

# Prep Assignment — Week 8 (Participation-Graded)

**Due:** Wednesday 18 November 2026, 12:00 — before the 13:00 seminar.
**Read first:** this week's lecture notes (`Lecture Week 8.md`) and the Reading Guide. The seminar discusses the
lecture; it does not introduce it, so the notes are part of the preparation.
Bring a printed or written copy; you will refer to it during discussion.

**Length:** Roughly one page. This is preparation, not an essay.

---

## Part A: Van Buren, in Your Own Words

In no more than 150 words, state what *Van Buren* decided and why it matters to someone doing security
research. **Do not quote the opinion.** If you cannot express it without quoting, you have not
finished reading it.

---

## Part B: Choose a Disclosure Policy

You are the first security hire at a 40-person company that has never received a vulnerability report.
Write the disclosure policy — **six bullet points maximum**. It must specify:

- Where reports are sent
- What you promise reporters (including whether you offer safe harbour, and in what terms)
- Your target timeline, and what happens if you miss it
- Whether you pay bounties, and how you would justify that decision to a sceptical CFO

Then add **one sentence** identifying the part of your own policy you are least confident about.

---

## Part C: The Line You Would Not Cross

Hacktivism arguments tend to be tested by extreme cases. Construct **one scenario** in which you think
unauthorised access would be ethically defensible, and **one** that is superficially similar but that
you think is not.

State, in a single sentence, **what feature distinguishes them.** This sentence is the actual
assignment; the scenarios exist to force you to produce it.

---

## What "Participation-Graded" Means Here

This prep work is assessed on evidence of **genuine engagement**, not on reaching the instructor's
view. A well-argued position the seminar leader disagrees with scores higher than a hedge that commits
to nothing.

Part C in particular has no expected answer. Students who find that their distinguishing feature
collapses under a follow-up question have done the assignment correctly — bring that to the seminar
and say so.

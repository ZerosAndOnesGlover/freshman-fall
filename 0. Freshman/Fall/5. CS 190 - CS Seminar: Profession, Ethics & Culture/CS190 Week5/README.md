### Data Collection, GDPR, and the Fourth Amendment

**Format:** Weekly 1-hour seminar + readings

### Why This Week Exists

Privacy harms rarely come from a single secret being exposed; they come from aggregation — many individually harmless facts combining into something the subject never disclosed. That structural difference is why privacy resists the intuitions that work elsewhere in this course.

### Learning Objectives

By the end of Week 5, you should be able to:

1. Explain why the 'privacy is about secrets' model fails to account for most modern privacy harms.
2. Describe surveillance capitalism as a business model and connect it to specific technical design choices.
3. Summarise the GDPR's core rights and the obligations they impose on a data controller.
4. Explain the third-party doctrine and how Carpenter v. United States altered it.
5. Apply data minimisation as a concrete engineering practice, not a slogan.

# Prep Assignment — Week 5 (Participation-Graded)

**Due:** Before seminar begins.
**Format:** One page, any format.
**Grading:** Credit/no-credit on completion and evident engagement.

---

## Part A: Solove Applied

Pick one app or service you use regularly. Identify one specific way it could plausibly cause each of the following three harms from Solove's taxonomy, even if you don't believe it currently does:

1. Aggregation
2. Secondary use
3. Exclusion (failing to let you know what data exists about you, or correct it)

One or two sentences per harm is sufficient — the goal is demonstrating you can apply the taxonomy to a real system, not writing an essay.

## Part B: Zuboff, Tested

In 4–6 sentences, apply Zuboff's surveillance capitalism framework to a specific free product or service. State whether you think the framework accurately describes that product's business logic, and why. Be specific about the product — "social media in general" is too vague; pick one.

## Part C: Carpenter's Reach

The lecture states *Carpenter v. United States* left open exactly how far its reasoning extends beyond cell-site location data. Pick one other category of data (browsing history, smart speaker recordings, fitness tracker biometric data, smart home device logs, or another of your choosing) and write 4–5 sentences arguing whether *Carpenter*'s logic — comprehensiveness plus practical unavoidability of generation — should extend to protect it under the Fourth Amendment. Take a position; don't just describe both sides.

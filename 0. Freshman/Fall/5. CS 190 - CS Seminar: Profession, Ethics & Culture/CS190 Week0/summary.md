# CS 190 · Week 0 · Summary

**Topic —** What is computer science? — the discipline before four years inside it
**Seminar —** One seminar: CS vs software engineering vs computer engineering, the careers landscape, and why ethics belongs in a CS curriculum.
**Work —** Prep Assignment (participation-graded): reading notes on Dijkstra, the ACM/IEEE Computing Curricula, and Russell & Norvig §1.1.
**Takeaway —** Three disciplines share one name and are told apart by their central question. Technical neutrality is a myth to test, not to assume — design choices embed values whether or not you intended them to.

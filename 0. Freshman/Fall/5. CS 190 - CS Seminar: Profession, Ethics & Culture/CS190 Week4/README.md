### Why This Week Exists

'Bias' is used loosely enough that it obscures more than it reveals. This week separates statistical bias from social bias, walks three documented failures end to end, and confronts the result that the major fairness criteria are mathematically incompatible — so 'just make it fair' is not an engineering instruction.

### Learning Objectives

By the end of Week 4, you should be able to:

1. Distinguish statistical bias, social bias, proxy variables, and selection bias precisely.
2. Reconstruct how the Amazon resume tool, credit-scoring systems, and Gender Shades each produced disparate outcomes, identifying the mechanism in each.
3. State demographic parity, equalised odds, and predictive parity, and explain why they cannot in general be satisfied simultaneously.
4. Explain the difference between disparate treatment and disparate impact as legal standards.

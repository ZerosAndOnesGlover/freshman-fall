# CS 190 · Week 4 · Summary

**Topic —** Algorithmic bias — hiring, lending, and facial recognition
**Seminar —** One seminar built on case studies where a deployed system produced discriminatory outcomes.
**Work —** Prep Assignment and seminar contribution.
**Takeaway —** Bias rarely enters as prejudice; it enters as a training set, a proxy variable, or an objective function nobody examined. "The model just learned the data" is a description of the failure, not a defence.

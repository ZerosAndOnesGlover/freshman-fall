# CS 190 · Week 11 · Summary

**Topic —** Student presentations on position-paper topics
**Seminar —** Three sessions; every student presents.
**Work —** **Week 11 Presentation** (10%, published rubric) and **Peer Feedback** (part of the 4% feedback and question-preparation component).
**Takeaway —** Defending an argument aloud, to people who have read the same sources, is a different skill from writing one — and the peer feedback is graded because receiving criticism well is part of it.

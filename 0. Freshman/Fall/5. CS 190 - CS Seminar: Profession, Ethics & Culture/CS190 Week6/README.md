## Week 6: AI and Society
### Autonomous Weapons, Deepfakes, and LLMs

**Format:** Weekly 1-hour seminar + readings
**Assessment for this course (overall):** Participation 40%, Position Papers 60%
**This week's deliverable:** **Position Paper #2** (750–1,000 words, Before Week 7 seminar) — plus the usual participation-graded prep notes

---

### Why This Week Exists

Week 4 examined systems that fail unfairly. This week examines systems whose *successful* operation is the concern — where the technology working exactly as designed is what raises the question. The accountability gap, not the error rate, is the through-line.

### Learning Objectives

By the end of Week 6, you should be able to:

1. Explain the accountability gap in autonomous weapons and why distinction and proportionality are difficult to guarantee mechanically.
2. Describe the liar's dividend and why detection alone cannot resolve the deepfake problem.
3. Distinguish task automation from job elimination when reasoning about LLM labour effects.
4. Analyse one AI technology in depth rather than three superficially — and defend that choice.

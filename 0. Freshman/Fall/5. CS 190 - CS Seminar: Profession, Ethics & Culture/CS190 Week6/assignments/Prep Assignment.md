## Part A: The Three Arguments, Mapped

For autonomous weapons, the lecture presents three distinct ethical arguments: harm-reduction (pro), accountability gap (con), and human dignity (con). In one sentence each, restate all three in your own words — not copied from the lecture — to confirm you understand the distinct logical structure of each (they are not interchangeable versions of "autonomous weapons are risky").

## Part B: Liar's Dividend, Applied

The "liar's dividend" argument holds that deepfakes harm society even when nobody is actually fooled by a specific fake, because the *existence* of convincing fakes lets guilty parties dismiss real evidence. Construct one hypothetical (or, if you're aware of one, real) scenario illustrating this dynamic, in 3–4 sentences. Be specific about who benefits from the dividend and what evidence gets discounted as a result.

This is provisional — you're allowed to change your mind in seminar — but arriving with no view means arriving unprepared to engage.

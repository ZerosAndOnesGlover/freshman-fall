# CS 190 · CS Seminar: Profession, Ethics & Culture
## Week 3: Ethics in Computing
### The ACM Code of Ethics and Professional Responsibility

**Format:** Weekly 1-hour seminar + readings
**Assessment for this course (overall):** Participation 40%, Position Papers 60%
**This week's deliverable:** **Position Paper #1** (750–1,000 words, Before Week 4 seminar) — plus the usual participation-graded prep notes

---

### Why This Week Exists

This is the hinge of the course. Weeks 0–2 built context; from here the seminar asks what you are obligated to do. The ACM Code is the profession's most explicit answer — and studying where it succeeds matters less than studying where it runs out.

### Learning Objectives

By the end of Week 3, you should be able to:

1. State the structure of the 2018 ACM Code of Ethics and apply its principles to a concrete scenario.
2. Identify at least two questions the Code does not resolve, and explain why they resist codification.
3. Evaluate the argument for mandatory licensure of software engineers, on both sides.
4. Write an argumentative position paper that takes and defends a stance rather than summarising.

### How This Course Is Graded (Reminder)

- **Participation (40%):** Weekly seminar attendance and engagement. Quality over quantity — one sharp, well-reasoned comment beats five reflexive ones.
- **Position Papers (60%):** Three 750–1,000 word argumentative papers, assigned Weeks 3, 6 and 9 (20% each). These are argumentative, not summary — you must take and defend a stance. The word limits are hard, and the papers escalate deliberately: #1 supplies the ACM Code as scaffold, #2 removes it, #3 removes the identifiable agent.

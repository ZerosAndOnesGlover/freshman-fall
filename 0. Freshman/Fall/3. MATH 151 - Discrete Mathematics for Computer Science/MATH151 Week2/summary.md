# MATH 151 · Week 2 · Summary

**Topic —** Proof techniques: direct, contrapositive, contradiction
**Lectures —** L06 Direct Proof; L07 Proof by Contrapositive; L08 Proof by Contradiction.
**Work —** PS 2, Lab 2 (proof workshop), Quiz 2 (covers Week 1).

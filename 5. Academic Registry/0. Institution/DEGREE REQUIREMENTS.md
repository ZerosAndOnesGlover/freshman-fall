### Graduation Requirements Checklist
- [ ] Minimum 142 credit hours completed
- [ ] Cumulative GPA ≥ 2.0
- [ ] CS Core GPA ≥ 2.0 (all CS/PROG courses)
- [ ] No more than two grades below C in required courses
- [ ] Senior Capstone completed with grade ≥ C
- [ ] All ethics seminars completed
- [ ] Applied for graduation by October 1 (spring graduation) or March 1 (summer/fall)
- [ ] Graduation audit cleared by academic advisor

> **⚠️ Open decisions on this calendar.**
> 1. **Public holidays.** Columbus Day (Mon Oct 12, W3), Veterans Day (Wed Nov 11, W7) and

>    is due on Christmas Day. Moving it to Wed Dec 23 or Thu Dec 24 is the cheapest fix.

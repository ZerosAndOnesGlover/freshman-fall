# CS 201 · Gradebook
## Computer Organization & Architecture · 4 credits · Year 2 Fall

> **How to use this file.** Enter a number in the **Earned** column only. Everything else —
> percentages, component subtotals, the course grade, the letter, and the GPA points — is computed by
> `tools/gpa.py`. Do not hand-edit the Computed block; it is overwritten on every run.
>
> Leave **Earned** blank for anything not yet marked. Blank rows are excluded from the average rather
> than counted as zero, so partial-term percentages stay meaningful. Enter `0` for a genuine zero and
> `EX` to excuse an item.

> **Weights come from the curriculum docx** — *Problem Sets 35%, Midterms 25%, Final 20%,
> Projects 20%* — and `MASTER TIMETABLE.md` states the same split. They sum to 100% **without a
> laboratory line**, so the thirteen labs are checked off in the session rather than marked, and the
> eleven quizzes are unmarked too. Both are recorded in `_CS 201 Lab and Quiz Record.md`, outside
> this file. See `1. Scheduling/Year2 - Sophomore/ASSESSMENT CALENDAR.md`.
>
> **Points convention.** Every problem set is out of 100; each exam and project is out of 100.

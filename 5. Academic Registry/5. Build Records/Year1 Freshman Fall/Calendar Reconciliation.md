## 1 · Why the Old Dates Were Wrong

The vault already carried a dated Year 1 Fall calendar — W1 = Sep 09, W6 midterms Oct 07–11,
W12 = Dec 02, finals Dec 16–20. Those dates are **internally consistent but belong to 2024**:

- The old [[ACADEMIC CALENDAR]] said *"Mon Sep 9 | Week 1 begins"*. **Sep 9 2026 is a Wednesday.**
  Sep 9 **2024** was a Monday.
- Likewise *"Wed Aug 28 | Classes begin"* — **Aug 28 2026 is a Friday.** Aug 28 **2024** was a
  Wednesday.

Every anchor lines up if the year is 2024 and none of them do for 2026. The old map was never
wrong for its own year; it was simply never re-derived when the term moved.

This also retires the `week-zero-is-ten-days` rule **for Year 1**: that rule described a
Wednesday-anchored ten-day Week 0, which cannot coexist with a Monday term start. See §5.

## 4 · Open Decisions

Neither was resolved, because both change the shape of the term rather than its arithmetic.

1. **Public holidays.** Labor Day (**Mon Sep 07**, in W3) and Veterans Day (**Wed Nov 11**, in W12)
   fall inside teaching weeks. The pre-2026 calendar observed Labor Day as NO CLASSES; this one
   currently runs classes on both, because the term was specified as thirteen continuous weeks.
   - Cancelling Mon Sep 07 costs W3's Monday lectures for MATH 141, MATH 151 and PHYS 141, their
     three quizzes, and the PROG 101 lab.
   - Cancelling Wed Nov 11 costs W12's CS 101 lecture and quiz.
2. **No reading day.** Instruction ends Fri Nov 13; finals open Mon Nov 16. The pre-2026 calendar
   allowed a reading day. Adding one pushes finals to Tue Nov 17 and the term end to Sat Nov 21.

### ⚠️ Unresolved: PROG 102's quiz day

All eleven PROG 102 quiz files say *"Week N · Monday, start of lecture"*. But [[Year1 - Freshman/SPRING SCHEDULE|SPRING SCHEDULE]]
puts PROG 102's lectures on **Tue/Wed/Thu** and its **lab** on Monday. A quiz at "the start of

This is **not** the same as the PROG 101 and MATH 142 conflicts, where course material named a real

the schedule wins by default: the calendar uses **Tuesday**, PROG 102's first lecture of the week
and the day the schedule already marks as its quiz day. The eleven files most likely inherited
"Monday" from the CS 102 template, which is genuinely Monday.

**Action needed:** either correct the eleven PROG 102 quiz headers to Tuesday, or confirm the
quizzes really are sat at the start of the Monday lab and reword them to say so.

## 4b · Lecture Dating (2026-08-16)

Every Year 1 lecture now carries a line of the form:

```
**Date:** Wednesday 2 September 2026 · 09:00–09:50 · Week 2
```

**340 of 343 lecture files dated and verified.** The three exceptions are in PROG 101's
`Appendix - Hashtables (not in curriculum)`, which has no week folder and is not taught.

Day and time were **derived**, not copied: the day from each course's meeting pattern in
`FALL/SPRING SCHEDULE.md` indexed by lecture position within the week, the time from the same
schedule (every course's slot is uniform across its meeting days), and the date from the
Week-to-Date Maps in §2 and §4a. Verification confirmed for all 340 that the named weekday
matches the actual date, the time matches the course's slot, and the date falls inside its
stated week.

## 5 · Convention Note

**Lectures carry dates; problem sets and labs do not.** The Week-to-Date Maps in
[[Year1 - Freshman/ASSESSMENT CALENDAR|ASSESSMENT CALENDAR]] remain the **source of truth**; the 340
lecture `**Date:**` lines are derived from them (§4b), not maintained independently. If the two
disagree, the map wins and the lecture lines get regenerated.

The original rule was *week numbers only, never dates*, because a dated file silently pins itself
to one calendar year — exactly how the 2024 dates survived into a 2026 term. That rule was
reversed by decision on 2026-08-16. The risk it guarded against is now spread across 340 files, so
**if a term ever moves, re-run the derivation rather than hand-editing.** The inputs are: the
Week-to-Date Map, each course's meeting days and time slot from `FALL/SPRING SCHEDULE.md`, and
lecture position within the week.

Both semesters of Year 1 are now dated. The inter-semester break runs **Sat 21 Nov 2026 →
Sun 10 Jan 2027** (Fall ends Fri Nov 20, Spring opens Mon Jan 11) — about seven weeks.

Years 2–5 are **not** dated and still carry their original dates, which the two failures above
suggest are also from earlier calendar years. Check each against its own anchors before trusting it.

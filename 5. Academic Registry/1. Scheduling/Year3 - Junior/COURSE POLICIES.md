# YEAR 3 COURSE POLICIES
### Junior · Computer Science & Engineering
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════


## Problem Set Policies

### Submission
- All problem sets submitted via the course portal before **17:00 on Friday**.
- Submissions are timestamped automatically. The portal clock is authoritative.
- **Format**: PDF only (unless code submission — see below). Handwritten solutions must be scanned clearly.
- **Code submissions**: Submit a `.zip` containing all source files + a [[5. Academic Registry/README|README]] with compilation and run instructions.
- **Naming convention**: `PS{N}_{LastName}_{StudentID}.pdf`

### Late Policy
| Lateness | Penalty |
|----------|---------|
| Submitted on time | Full credit |
| Up to 24 hours late | −10% |
| 24–48 hours late | −20% |
| 48–72 hours late | −30% |
| More than 72 hours late | 0 credit |

**Extension requests**: Email instructor ≥ 48 hours before the deadline. Include reason and proposed new deadline. Extensions are not guaranteed. If granted, the extension is for one problem set at a time — not blanket extensions.

### Collaboration Policy
- **Discussing** problem approaches: ✅ Encouraged
- **Sharing notes** from lecture: ✅ OK
- **Working through examples together** in office hours: ✅ OK
- **Showing your code** to another student: ❌ Honor violation
- **Looking at another student's written solution**: ❌ Honor violation
- **Posting solutions online** (even after the course): ❌ Honor violation
- **Using AI to generate your solution**: ❌ Unless explicitly permitted

**Collaboration acknowledgment**: Include at the top of each problem set: "I worked on this problem set independently." OR "I discussed problem [N] with [Name(s)] — the write-up is my own."

## Lab Policies

### Attendance
- Labs are **mandatory**. Each unexcused absence reduces your final course grade by one letter after the second absence.
- **Excused absences**: Notify the TA and instructor via email before the lab starts (except genuine emergencies). Documentation required for medical absences.
- **Late arrival**: More than 15 minutes late counts as a partial absence. Two partial absences = one full absence.

### Lab Conduct
- Come prepared: read the lab handout before arriving. Labs are not designed for you to read the handout at the start.
- Lab checkoffs: TAs will ask you to demonstrate your work or explain your approach. Correct output alone is insufficient.
- No working on other courses during lab time.
- Help your neighbors — collaborative problem-solving during labs is encouraged and expected.

### Lab Reports
- Lab reports submitted by the end of the following day (Friday labs → Monday 23:59) unless otherwise noted.
- Reports are graded on: correctness, understanding (can you explain your results?), and completeness.

## Quiz Policies

- Quizzes held at the **start of Monday lecture**, Weeks 2–14 (12 quizzes).
- Duration: 10 minutes. Begin exactly at lecture start. No extensions for late arrivals.
- Material: previous week's lectures + assigned readings.
- **Lowest 2 quiz scores dropped** from final calculation.
- No make-up quizzes except for documented excused absences (Dean's excuse or equivalent).
- Quizzes are closed-book, closed-notes, no calculator (unless specified).

## Exam Policies

### Permitted Materials (Standard — may vary per course)
| Item | Midterm | Final |
|------|---------|-------|
| Handwritten notes | 1 side of one 8.5×11" sheet | 2 sides of one 8.5×11" sheet |
| Calculator | No (unless stated) | No (unless stated) |
| Printed materials | No | No |
| Electronic devices | No | No |

### During the Exam
- Student ID required at every exam. No ID = no exam.
- Arrive ≥ 10 minutes early to find seating.
- No leaving the exam room in the first 15 minutes or last 10 minutes.
- Questions: raise your hand — proctors will come to you.
- If you finish early, review your work. You may leave quietly after the first 15 minutes.

### After the Exam
- Exam solutions posted on course portal within 48 hours.
- Graded exams returned within 2 weeks.
- Re-grade requests: in writing, within 7 calendar days of return. See University Policies.

## Project Policies

### Team Projects (where applicable)
- Teams of indicated size; team formation instructions provided at project release.
- **Peer evaluation**: Each team member evaluates contribution of all other members. Peer eval scores may adjust individual grades ±10%.
- **All team members are responsible for all parts of the project**. "I didn't write that part" is not an excuse for not understanding it during demos.

### Project Demos
- Live demos to course staff during scheduled demo hours.
- Demo slots booked via portal ≥ 3 days in advance.
- Demo duration: 10–20 minutes depending on project complexity.
- Staff will ask you to demonstrate specific functionality and explain your implementation choices.

### Academic Integrity in Projects
- All code must be written by the project team.
- External libraries: permitted (with citation in README). Must understand what the library does.
- Open-source code: permitted as a dependency. Cannot copy and claim as your own work.
- AI-generated code: must disclose in README. Must be able to explain every line.

## Grading Disputes

1. Wait at least 24 hours after receiving your grade before initiating a dispute (read the rubric carefully first).
2. Submit a written re-grade request to the instructor via email:
   - Subject: `[CS XXX] Re-grade Request — PS/Quiz/Exam N — [Your Name]`
   - Body: Specific questions contested, with reasoning.
3. The instructor will review within 7 business days.
4. If still unsatisfied: escalate to the Department Academic Affairs Coordinator (not the Department Chair).

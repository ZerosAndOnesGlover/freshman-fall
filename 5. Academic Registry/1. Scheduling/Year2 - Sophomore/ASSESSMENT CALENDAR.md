## What Counts, and What Does Not

Two kinds of work in Year 2 are **required but unweighted**, and they are marked here so that you
know to do them and know not to look for them in your grade:

- **🧪 Labs.** Every Year 2 course's stated components sum to 100% without a laboratory line —
  CS 201 is Problem Sets 35 + Midterms 25 + Final 20 + Projects 20, and the rest are the same shape.
  Labs are checked off by the TA in the session. They are enforced by the attendance rule in
  `COURSE POLICIES.md`, not by a mark. *(CS 102 in Year 1 works the same way.)*

  **A lab is filed under the week whose material it covers, which is usually not the week it is
  sat.** Where a course's lab day falls before that week's lectures have finished, **Lab *N* is sat
  in Week *N+1*** — true of CS 201, whose lectures are Mon/Wed/Fri and whose lab is Tuesday. **Week 0
  labs are the exception everywhere**: they are setup sessions and sit on the Friday that closes the
  ten-day Week 0. Each course's syllabus states its own mapping, and every lab file carries its
  sitting week in the header.
- **📊 Quizzes.** Ten minutes at the start of each course's first lecture of the week, Weeks 1–11.
  **Quiz *N* covers Week *N−1*.** The answer key is printed in the paper.

Both are recorded per course in `2. Gradebook/Year2 Sophomore/<term>/_<COURSE> Lab and Quiz Record.md`,
whose leading underscore keeps `tools/gpa.py` from scanning them as courses.

week's own assignment list. This calendar previously stacked all four Fall midterms into Week 6 and
all of the second midterms into Week 10, with wider coverage than the docx claims. The docx wins —

`0. Institution/ACADEMIC CALENDAR.md` has been brought into line.

## Exam Conflict Resolution

If two exams fall on the same evening:
1. Notify your academic advisor AND both instructors within 48 hours of receiving the schedule.
2. The course with fewer enrolled students typically offers an alternate sitting.
3. Alternate sittings are offered on a different evening (typically the following morning).
4. You must complete both exams — you cannot skip one.

## Re-grade Requests

Submit in writing to the course instructor within **7 calendar days** of grade release. Include:
- Your name and student ID
- The specific question(s) you are contesting
- A clear explanation of why you believe re-grading is warranted
- Your original submitted work (if electronic, the timestamp in the portal serves as evidence)

Note: The **entire** submission is re-evaluated. Grades may go up or down.

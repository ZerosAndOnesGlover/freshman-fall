## Exam Conflict Resolution

If two exams fall on the same evening:
1. Notify your academic advisor AND both instructors within 48 hours of receiving the schedule.
2. The course with fewer enrolled students typically offers an alternate sitting.
3. Alternate sittings are offered on a different evening (typically the following morning).
4. You must complete both exams, you cannot skip one.

## Re-grade Requests

Submit in writing to the course instructor within **7 calendar days** of grade release. Include:
- Your name and student ID
- The specific question(s) you are contesting
- A clear explanation of why you believe re-grading is warranted
- Your original submitted work (if electronic, the timestamp in the portal serves as evidence)

Note: The **entire** submission is re-evaluated. Grades may go up or down.

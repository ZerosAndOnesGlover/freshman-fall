## Communication Etiquette

| Channel | Use For | Expected Response Time |
|---------|---------|----------------------|
| Email | Logistics, extensions, excused absences | 48 hours (business days) |
| Course Forum (Ed/Piazza) | Technical questions, concept clarification | 24 hours (instructors + TAs monitor) |
| Office Hours | Complex questions, debugging, exam prep | Immediate (during OH) |
| Slack/Discord (if provided) | Quick clarifications | Best effort (not guaranteed) |

> **Do not email homework solutions or exam questions.** Post anonymously on the course forum instead.

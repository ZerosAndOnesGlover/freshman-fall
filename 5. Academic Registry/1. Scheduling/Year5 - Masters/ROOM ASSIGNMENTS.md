## Campus Quick Reference

Graduate courses are held primarily in the Lovelace Graduate Center, with electives that need specialized equipment (ML systems, embedded/reliability labs) borrowing space in the undergraduate buildings.

| Building | Abbreviation | Primary Use |
|----------|-------------|------------|
| Lovelace Graduate Center | LGC | M.S.S.E. lectures, seminar rooms, advisor offices |
| Turing Hall | TH | Overflow lectures, shared with undergrad CS |
| Babbage Hall | BH | Shared labs, help desk |
| Von Neumann Center | VNC | Large lecture halls (exams, SE 500 colloquium overflow) |
| Maxwell Engineering Complex | MEC | ECE/embedded labs (used by SE 542) |
| Main Library | LIB | Silent study, thesis/project writing carrels |

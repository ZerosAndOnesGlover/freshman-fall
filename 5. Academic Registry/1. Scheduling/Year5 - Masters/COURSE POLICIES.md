# YEAR 5 COURSE POLICIES
### M.S. Software Engineering
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════


## Track Selection

The program has three tracks, chosen no later than the start of M2 Fall:

| Track | Culminating Experience | Credits |
|-------|------------------------|---------|
| **Thesis** | Original research contribution (SE 599A/B), faculty-supervised, formal defense before a 3-person committee | 6 |
| **Project** | Substantial engineering project (SE 590A/B), built + evaluated to graduate standard, public presentation | 6 |
| **Coursework** | 2 additional electives in lieu of thesis/project (e.g. SE 550, SE 560) | 6 |

Track changes after M2 Fall begins require Program Office approval and may extend time-to-degree.

---

## Problem Set Policies

### Submission
- Submitted via the course portal before **17:00** on the due date specified per course.
- **Format**: PDF (write-ups) or `.zip` with [[5. Academic Registry/README|README]] (code submissions), same convention as undergraduate courses.
- **Naming convention**: `PS{N}_{LastName}_{StudentID}.pdf`

### Late Policy
| Lateness | Penalty |
|----------|---------|
| Submitted on time | Full credit |
| Up to 48 hours late | −15% |
| 48–96 hours late | −30% |
| More than 96 hours late | 0 credit |

**Extension requests**: Email instructor ≥ 72 hours before the deadline. Graduate extensions are evaluated individually — cite specific circumstances (research, TA-ship, or advisor obligations are valid reasons).

### Collaboration Policy
- **Discussing** approaches and readings: ✅ Encouraged — graduate seminars are built around discussion.
- **Co-authoring problem sets**: ❌ Unless explicitly assigned as a team exercise.
- **Using AI to generate your solution**: ❌ Unless explicitly permitted by the instructor. Research proposals, theses, and project reports must be your own analysis and writing.

---

## Seminar & Colloquium Attendance (SE 500A–D)

- **SE 500A (Research Colloquium)**: mandatory attendance, all 4 semesters. 2 unexcused absences per semester max before it affects your standing.
- **SE 500B (Career Practicum)**: mandatory, Spring Y1 and Fall Y2 only.
- **SE 500C / SE 500D**: one-time milestone events (thesis/project proposal and defense) — attendance and presentation are pass/fail components of your track requirement, not a seminar series.
- These are 0-credit but **required for degree conferral** — missing them without an approved excuse delays graduation regardless of coursework GPA.

| Calculator / electronic devices | No (unless stated) | No (unless stated) |

### During the Exam

- Some graduate courses (e.g. SE 505, SE 506) substitute a final presentation for a written exam — check the course syllabus.

---

## Course & Research Project Policies

- **Team projects** (SE 502 System Project, SE 504 Team Project): peer evaluation adjusts individual grades ±10%, same as undergraduate policy.
- **Thesis/Project (SE 599/590)**: advisor sets milestone deadlines individually within the Fall Y2 (proposal) → Spring Y2 (defense) timeline in ASSESSMENT CALENDAR.md. Missed advisor meetings without notice count against your progress evaluation.
- **AI-generated code/text**: must be disclosed in any submission (problem set, project, thesis chapter). You must be able to explain and defend every part of your own work — this is tested directly at thesis/project defense.

---

## Grading Disputes

1. Wait 24 hours after receiving your grade, and re-read the rubric.
2. Submit a written re-grade request to the instructor: `[SE XXX] Re-grade Request — Assessment N — [Your Name]`.
3. Instructor reviews within 7 business days.
4. Unresolved disputes escalate to the M.S.S.E. Program Director (not the Department Chair).
5. Thesis/project committee decisions (proposal pass/fail, defense outcome) are not subject to the standard re-grade process — appeals go through the Graduate Studies Committee.

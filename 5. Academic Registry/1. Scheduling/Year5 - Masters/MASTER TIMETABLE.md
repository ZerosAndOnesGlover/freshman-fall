## Program Overview

Two-year, 4-semester, 36-credit-hour program. Thesis Track shown as the default path below (SE 599A/B) — Project Track substitutes SE 590A/B in the same slot; Coursework Track drops the thesis/project block entirely and adds 2 more electives instead (see COURSE POLICIES.md).

Required Core (18 credits, 6 courses) is fixed by semester. Technical Electives (12–18 credits, 3–4+ courses) are chosen from a 12-course menu spread across all four semesters — one representative elective per semester is shown below with a real schedule; the rest of the menu exists as reference folders only (see ROOM ASSIGNMENTS.md for the "elective slot" note).

## Weekly Rhythm

Graduate courses meet 2× 75 minutes/week (Format per docx), not the daily 50-min undergrad pattern — expect fewer contact hours and far more independent reading/research time between sessions.

| Day | Standard Events |
|-----|----------------|
| **Monday** | Lecture/seminar block 1 for MW-paired courses. |
| **Tuesday** | Lecture/seminar block 1 for TTh-paired courses (electives). |
| **Wednesday** | Lecture/seminar block 2 for MW-paired courses. Research Colloquium (SE 500A) 17:00. |
| **Thursday** | Lecture/seminar block 2 for TTh-paired courses. Career Practicum (SE 500B) 17:00 where applicable. |
| **Friday** | Thesis/Project advisor meeting (M2 only), 14:00–15:50. Biweekly SE 503 lab (M1 Spring only). |
| **Saturday/Sunday** | Independent research, reading, and writing. No scheduled sessions. |

## Assessment Types and Weights (M.S.S.E. Standard)

| Type | Frequency | Weight | Notes |
|------|-----------|--------|-------|
| **Problem Sets** | Weekly or biweekly | 25–35% | Individual write-up unless a team project is specified. |
| **Midterm Exam** | Mid-semester | 25–30% | Evening exam, most core courses. |
| **Final Exam / Presentation** | Finals week | 20–25% | Written exam or public presentation depending on course. |
| **Course Project** | 1 per semester | 15–40% | Architecture doc, system build, quality audit, RFC, or research proposal depending on course. |
| **Thesis/Project Milestones** | Ongoing, Year 2 | Pass/fail | Proposal (Fall Y2) → Defense (Spring Y2), advisor-assessed. |

## Study Load Estimate

The **2-hour rule** applies loosely at the graduate level — expect 3+ hours of independent work per contact hour for research-heavy courses (SE 505, SE 599/590).

| Semester | Credits | In-Class Hrs/Week | Study/Research Hrs/Week | Total Hrs/Week |
|----------|---------|--------------------|--------------------------|-----------------|
| M1 Fall | 9 | 6 | 20 | 26 |
| M1 Spring | 9 | 6.5 | 20 | 26.5 |
| M2 Fall | 9 | 5.5 | 22 | 27.5 |
| M2 Spring | 9 | 6 | 22 | 28 |

> Lower contact hours than undergrad, but the reading load and thesis/project research time more than make up for it. Plan accordingly.

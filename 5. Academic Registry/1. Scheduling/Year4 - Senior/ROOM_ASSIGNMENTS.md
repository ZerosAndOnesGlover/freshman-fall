## Exam Room Assignments

Exam rooms are larger than lecture rooms to allow distanced seating.
Assignments posted on the course portal ≥ 1 week before each exam.

| Exam | Typical Venue | Notes |
|------|--------------|-------|
| Midterm Exams | VNC 100 (Main Auditorium, 500 seats) | All courses share this space; different evenings |
| Final Exams | VNC 100 + overflow to TH 200 | Assignment based on course enrollment |
| Make-up Exams | TH 105 (proctored testing center) | By appointment with instructor |

# PROG 201 · Week 4 · Summary

**Topic —** Memory-mapped files and the virtual memory API: `mmap`, demand paging, where `malloc` gets memory, `mprotect`, JIT compilation, huge pages.
**Lectures —** L13 `mmap`, and What a Mapping Costs; L14 Where Memory Comes From: `brk`, `mmap`, and `malloc`; L15 `mprotect`, Writing Machine Code, and Huge Pages.
**Work —** **Midterm 1** (Monday, 18:00–19:30, Weeks 0–3, 12.5%), PS 4 (100, due Friday of Week 5), Quiz 4 (Tuesday, covers Week 3, unmarked). **Lab 3 is sat on Monday of this week; Lab 4 covers this week and is sat on Monday of Week 5.**
**Takeaway —** Every region of a process is an `mmap`, and `/proc/pid/maps` is the list. Address space is free and memory is not: **4 GiB mapped cost 1 MiB of RSS**, reading a gigabyte of it cost nothing because untouched anonymous pages resolve to one shared zero page, writing it cost exactly 1,024 MiB, and `MADV_DONTNEED` gave it all back **and destroyed the data**. A minor fault is **1.9 µs** against 19 ns for a store to a resident page, and a major fault is **563 µs** — but readahead means order decides everything, with the same cold 256 MiB taking **0.17 s sequentially and 1.58 s randomly**. File mappings fault **132 pages at a time**; anonymous ones exactly one. `mmap` beat a 1 MiB-buffered `read` by 8× for sparse access because `read` copies, and loses for streaming. **There is no heap** — `malloc` is a library function, `brk` moves one number, and glibc's `mmap` threshold is documented as 128 KiB and **measured at 134,473 bytes**. A 150-line allocator beat glibc **0.88×** and **0.48×** on two workloads and lost **339×** on a fragmenting one, which is a fact about the workload. `mprotect` plus a `SIGSEGV` handler gives a guard page and a write barrier — **7 stores to 4 pages cost 4 faults**. A JIT is **17 bytes and an `mprotect`**, and beats a bytecode VM **5.5×** while merely tying the C compiler. W^X fails safe in three directions and **`PROT_WRITE|PROT_EXEC` is simply allowed here**. Huge pages: 42.7 → 32.3 ns, **1.32×**, with only 114 MiB of 512 promoted. And `SIGSEGV` means not mapped; **`SIGBUS` means mapped with nothing behind it** — including a file truncated under a live mapping.

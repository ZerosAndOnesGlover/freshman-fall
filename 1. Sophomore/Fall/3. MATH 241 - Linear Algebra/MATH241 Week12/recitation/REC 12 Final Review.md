## 1. One Matrix, Twelve Weeks (20 min)

$$A = \begin{bmatrix}5&0&-2\\ 0&3&-2\\ -2&-2&4\end{bmatrix}$$

**This is Week 10's matrix.** Every answer below is in L30–L32 — **do not look until your pair has finished**, then check.

**In pairs, one part each, rotating the pen every part:**

**(a) [Week 1]** Eliminate. Give the pivots and $L$. **Write $A = LDL^\mathsf{T}$.**

**(b) [Week 5]** $\det A$ from the pivots.

**(c) [Week 3]** Rank, and a basis for each of the four subspaces. *(Short answer — say why it is short.)*

**(d) [Week 6]** Verify that $(1,2,2)$, $(2,1,-2)$, $(2,-2,1)$ are eigenvectors and give the eigenvalues. **Check trace and determinant.**

**(e) [Week 7]** Give $A^{10}$'s eigenvalues and $\operatorname{trace}A^{10}$ **without multiplying any matrices.**

**(f) [Week 8]** Build $Q$. What is $Q^{-1}$, and what is $\operatorname{cond}(Q)$?

**(g) [Week 10]** Is $A$ positive definite? **Answer by three different tests** from work already on your paper.

**(h) [Week 11]** Give the SVD of $A$. **Why is it so short?** *(And what would change if one eigenvalue were negative?)*

**(i) [Week 11]** $\lVert A\rVert_2$, $\lVert A\rVert_F$, $\operatorname{cond}_2(A)$ — and **check $\lVert A\rVert_F^2$ from the entries.**

**(j) [Week 12]** $A$ is a covariance matrix. **What fraction of the variance does the first principal direction explain, and which direction is it?**

> **The point of (a)–(j).** Ten weeks, ten questions, **one matrix**, and each answer checked the
> previous one. **On Monday, look for the same thing**: a number from one part of a question that
> checks another.

**(f)** Find the best rank-$20$ approximation of a $1000\times800$ image.
